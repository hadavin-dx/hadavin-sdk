Review by the author, or by a reviewer primed with the expected answer, does not count as reviewed. Give the reviewer the diff and the spec only. Subagent reports are unverified until checked. Security, money and redaction paths: repeat independent rounds until one finds nothing real (owner decision 8, 2026-11-01); other work 2 rounds.
