Owner, 2026-10-31: AI projects fail for lack of clear, in-depth research into client problems, common use cases, how AI goes wrong, model behaviour, cost and accuracy. Hadavin must be different: perfect at the most common use cases, with a step-by-step continuous process, multiple checks and tests, secure architecture, guardrails and approvals, and no destruction of client data, systems or workflows.

**Honest framing agreed in chat:** LLMs can never be guaranteed hallucination-free. The promise is instead:
1. mistakes cannot cause damage: grounding checks, policy at the tool gateway, approvals, least privilege, dry runs, undo;
2. mistakes are measured and caught: accuracy per project on the client's data, abstaining when unsure, continuous evals including on model change;
3. autonomy is earned: shadow, then suggest, then act per action type.

**How to apply:** every design, build and delivery is judged against this. The research "Reliable agentic AI delivery" feeds the Hadavin Reliability Method and the core v4 design. Related: [[best-over-rules]]
