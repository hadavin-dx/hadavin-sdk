Owner 2026-11-04: "if they are not perfect, build on top of them to make them perfect; don't start from scratch again; whatever they lack you need to find a way to fix, not replace them entirely."

**Why:** restarts waste days and tokens; most pieces (video tools, design notes, WP modules) are mostly right and fail in specific places.
**How to apply:** before proposing a rewrite, list what the existing piece lacks and fix those gaps (tool ladder: reuse, then extend, then replace only with evidence and the owner's yes). A replacement needs a written reason the gaps cannot be fixed. [[tool-ladder-before-code]] [[agent-speed-estimates]]
