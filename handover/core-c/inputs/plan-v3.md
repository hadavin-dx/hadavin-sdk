- Cut: the landing set is the review-clean PREFIX of the A2 order when the lead calls the cut; the cut is called when every session has either met phase-2 exit for all its items or is on an escalated item for more than two lead ticks with no other READY item; the confirming run and the equality proofs are on that prefix.
