The plan's marker 2 says the owner decides; decision 7 says the owner only types `y`, the challenge and `end`. 2. **L2 (who is asked).** The checklist says "tell the builder" in the channel (the studio session that gave the lead the file). If that window does not exist, the checklist says: stay stopped, save the lines in `stop-notes.txt`, ask whoever gave you the file. Needed from the lead: name the person or window that really answers (and confirm D2 may refer to it).
