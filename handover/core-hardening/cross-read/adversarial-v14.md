What I did: read the three v14 files in full, the v13 list, v14-CHANGES.md (first 60 lines), and grepped DECISIONS.md for the "same id / lease / one Postgres" wording. I checked dates, weekday names, the UTC to IST conversions and the worked-example arithmetic by hand. Nothing was run.

## A. Claims that the film or post would make without evidence behind them

1. SHEET:147 and SHEET:55 against APP:102 item 8: the caption "one Postgres server, two databases" has no evidence step. SHEET:147 tells the owner to "say" it. APP:102 item 8 says it is "not proven until the rehearsal shows it". Nothing tells the owner how to learn that the rehearsal showed it. Go item 9 (SHEET:55) is only the studio lead's statement, and the database names are blank B3. The shot list (APP:133) has no scene for it. Fix: the caption is allowed only when the studio lead's written rehearsal evidence names the file and line that shows two databases on one server. Until then SHEET:147 must say "do not caption it".
2. SHEET:144: "the printed result shows it: person A was sent twice and got one contact and one mail". The invented sample result (APP:67-70) prints "Person A: 1 contact, 1 mail" and "Sends made: 13". Nothing prints that A was sent twice, or that it was the same person id. So the printed result shows "one contact, one mail", not "sent twice". Fix: require a printed line that states "Person A sends: 2, same person id", or have the studio lead's written statement name where that fact is shown. Otherwise the claim has only the studio lead's word.
3. SHEET:144: A and B are both addressed at fixed `take-<run>-a@example.com` addresses. HubSpot already combines contacts with the same email address. So "1 contact" does not prove that the program processed the lead once. The "1 mail" count is the real evidence. Fix: say so in J; the claim is backed by the mail count only, and the post must not point at the contact list as proof.
4. SHEET:144 (the claim) with APP:133 S4: the shot list has a scene S4 "receiver mail LIST under label demo", but no step on the card or sheet makes it happen (CARD:58-62 films only the HubSpot contact list). The one piece of evidence for "processed once" would not be on camera. Fix: add an F.1 step "film the receiver mail list under label demo, expect exactly 3 or 2 mails", with who films, which profile and which tab.
5. APP:133 S2 against SHEET:112 (C.2) and CARD:53: S2 films "n8n's list of runs during the sends". The `demo-take` profile is told to sign in only to HubSpot and the receiver (SHEET:112). n8n is signed in only in the normal profile (SHEET:113). During the sends the studio lead shuts n8n down and starts it again (CARD:53). So S2 is either a sign-in page or an error page. Fix: delete S2, or move it to the end of the run, and add the n8n sign-in to C.2.
6. SHEET:147 and APP:102 apply the list of things not to say only to "the post". The video's captions, voice-over, thumbnail and title have no such list. The film shows the owner typing `y` and a 6-digit number (CARD:49-50), which a viewer reads as an approval step. APP:102 item 4 bans that claim only in the post. Fix: say the ban list and the narrow claim apply to every spoken or written word in the video as well.
7. APP:67-70 and CARD:61: the printed result with "Person C: unknown" is filmed (S5, APP:133). A viewer sees a failure the post says nothing about. This is a choice for the owner, but the text does not say "check that the film does not show C unknown, or accept it". Fix: add it to the H list (SHEET:126).
8. APP:132 item 5 and SHEET:9: the proof screen is "made by the studio lead from the rehearsal". A rehearsal picture inside a video of the real run has no label saying "from the rehearsal". Fix: the caption on that picture reads "rehearsal, made-up data" (or the picture is dropped).
9. SHEET:145 (boxed lease sentence): the text is in front of the owner to copy. It claims "up to three tries in total", which nothing on the card, in the printed result or in the film shows. It is also described as differing from the studio lead's own ruling (a contradiction the owner cannot resolve). SHEET:144 says "nothing else about repeats or crashes", and the box then offers a crash sentence. Fix: move the box out of the owner's sheet into the studio lead's notes, and leave one line in J: "no sentence about crashes or lease unless the studio lead sends the exact sentence in writing".
10. SHEET:144: "the studio lead confirmed both in writing". "Both" has two readings (A and B; or the exit code and the counts). Fix: "confirmed in writing: exit code accepted, A one and one, B one and one".

## B. Costs money, leaks a key, or leaves a live system behind

11. CARD:48 against SHEET:60 and APP:49 (blank B10): the notice check says type `n` if "people, calls or mails are more than 3". The call ceiling in the money rule may be higher than 3 if the flows can call again (B10). A correct notice with ceiling 4 or 6 makes the owner refuse, or the owner changes the number in the head. Fix: "calls are more than the call ceiling in the blanks".
12. CARD:24, CARD:48: "price per call is not the blank above". No rounding rule: A.2's calculator can print 0.30000000000000004 where the notice prints 0.3, and the notice may print 0.27 or 0.2700. Fix: say "same figure to 2 decimal places counts as equal; anything else: `n`".
13. CARD:60 and CARD:61 against CARD:31 and rule 1 (SHEET:73): Freeze says words only, never copied text. F.3 says "send the number and the last printed line", F.4 says "send the printed result". F.3 is exactly a Freeze case. The last printed line may hold an address or a path. Fix: F.3 and F.4 say "words only: the number, and 'the last line says <paraphrase>'; copy no address or path".
14. CARD:61 (F.4: cancel the key now, "do not wait for the studio lead") against APP:91 row 15 (the studio lead shuts down the model program after) and CARD:55 ("the model program keeps running during the film pause"). The key is cancelled while the model program or n8n may still be finishing person C (unknown). Nothing on the card says n8n's flows are switched off before F.5 or I.0. The studio lead shuts n8n down only in I.5, after H (SHEET:136). Meanwhile APP:55 and SHEET:145 say recovery can pick up unfinished leads after the lease. A pickup after F.5 creates a new contact and mail that the owner's list never held, and it uses the live HubSpot take key and the sender login. Fix: F.5 starts only after the studio lead writes "flows are off" (or after the lease length on row 3 has passed with no new contact), and the contact list in F.5 is re-counted once more at I.5.
15. SHEET:93 (0.6) says the owner deletes a leftover step 0 contact "before step D". CARD:62 (F.5) and APP:117 say the list for F.5 "includes any leftover contact of step 0", which happens after the film. Two orders. If the second is followed the contact is filmed (APP:132 item 9 forbids that). Fix: pick one, 0.6 "before step D", and delete the step 0 part from the F.5 and step G text. Also, F.5 says "search the run name", which will not find a step 0 contact whose address is a different made-up address; say "search that address".
16. SHEET:92 (0.5): the filter `[A-Za-z0-9_-]{30,}` also matches any long PASS line made of letters, underscores and hyphens (a long test label is enough). A hit means "Key exposed: delete the HubSpot probe app", which is heavy and not undoable. Fix: say first "send the count and ask; do not delete anything for a count unless the studio lead confirms a key-like string", or make the filter match only a string starting with the known probe-key prefix that the studio lead names on row 19. I cannot check what the real result file holds.
17. SHEET:8 (the key lives until the end of the run day or 24 hours after making it, whichever is first) and SHEET:97 (step A "on the evening before"): no step compares the key's 24-hour end with row 3's latest start plus the run length plus I.0. A key made at 20:00 the evening before, a run starting at 19:00 the next day and a 15-minute second reading cannot end before the 24 hours. Fix: row 3 gets one more blank: "key made at ... so the key ends at ...; the run must finish before that".
18. SHEET:8 and SHEET:122: "the end of the run day" and "the same day" give no time zone. The run date is in UTC and IST (SHEET:3) and a run at 20:00 UTC is already the next day in India. Fix: "the deadline is the UTC and IST time on row 11; if the two differ, the earlier one counts".
19. CARD:48 stops at `n` with no next step. After `n` the model program is still running and the key is valid (CARD:48 itself says so). CARD:71 says "I.0 same day", but never says "after `n`: cancel the key unless you will restart within <minutes>". It also does not say whether the run name and the lock file are used (APP:105 says exit code 2 refuses a used run name). Fix: one line "after `n`: send 'refused at the notice'; the studio lead says in writing whether the name can be used again; the key stays until I.0".
20. CARD:3, SHEET:80 (rule 11): the run window may hold only `cd`, `clear`, the start line, `y`, the confirmation line, `end`, `echo $?`. CARD:48 and CARD:33 tell the owner to type `n`. `n` is not on the list. Fix: add `n`.

The studio lead can satisfy go item 3 and the owner cannot satisfy A.6. Also SHEET:85 says step 0 runs only after the patch of blank B5; without the patch, step 0 never runs and "paths work" never exists. Fix: one rule: go item 3 and A.6 say the same words.
22. APP:60-62 and CARD:48 against APP:63: if the notice has no line for masked accounts (APP:63, SHEET:105 last line) the owner must type `n` unless the go message says otherwise; but if the notice is also missing the "window end" line the owner is told only "you cannot find a line: `n`". ## C. Steps in the wrong order, done twice, or without a stop condition
