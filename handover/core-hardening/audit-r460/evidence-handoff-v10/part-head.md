The only hard stop for a hostile process is a spend limit set at the provider (S-3, owner, POST-DEMO). A process holding the runtime database role can also change the spend tables directly (including another agent's rows). No sentence here says "no unmetered spend" or "cannot overspend"; section 6 lists every paid path.
