## 1. What happens, and the words
**The whole run in ten lines.** (1) You choose a run name and tell the lead. (2) The lead gives you the numbers (the numbers message); you check them by hand and type a yes. (3) The lead says "go". (4) You make a fresh paid-model key and save it in a file the lead's model program reads: from now the key is **alive**, which means it can spend money until you cancel it. (5) You start the test program; it shows a notice; you compare it and type y and a number. (6) The test program submits three made-up people to n8n, which makes one contact and one mail per person and asks the paid model at most once per person. (7) A film person films the contact list and the mail list. (8) You type `end`, then at once cancel the key with the card and prove it. (9) The lead tells you what to delete and revoke; you do it by hand. (10) The film is checked; a sentence is posted if everything agrees.
