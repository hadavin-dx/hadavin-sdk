### Start, stop, restart, kill, cleanup
- Start: kinds built and probed at start-up (signer `probe`, GCS/Azure anchor probes, Bedrock `check_filter`, chain table check chain.py:`build`). S3 anchor is not probed (A-08).
- Stop/kill: no background thread of its own except the 8 resolver threads (http.py:123, daemon, die with the process). A kill between `record_decision` and `on_decided` is covered by tick reconcile (tick.py:85-97). A kill during `execute` leaves the run EXECUTING; tick sets NEEDS_PERSON `outcome_unknown` (tick.py:117-121).
- Restart: `_pending` events and `_unanchored` anchors are lost (A-01, A-07); the outbox table or folder survives but is never read back (A-01).
- Cleanup: no purge of audit rows (by design append-only) or of audit_outbox or local `outbox/corrupt/`; no retention setting found for them (UNVERIFIED; D3 to check docs).

### What a new person must know
