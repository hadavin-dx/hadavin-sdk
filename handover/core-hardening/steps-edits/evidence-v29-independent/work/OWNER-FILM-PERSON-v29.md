# Film person page (v29): for the film person only; the same for sheet A and sheet B
You are the **film person**: a human helper who films two browser pages for the account owner. You are not the owner and you never hold the owner's keys or passwords. The owner reads the long sheet; you read only this page. Times are UTC with IST (IST = UTC + 5:30).
1. **What you may touch.** Only the browser window of the Chrome profile `demo-take` (the film profile) and the screen recording tool the owner names. Never the test terminal (a black Ubuntu window), never the model account, never another profile, never a key, never a password.
2. **When.** You start only at the film cue, when the owner says the film pause begins (it lasts 10 minutes). Before that, wait where the owner puts you; the owner may be alone at a screen for a while: that is normal.
3. **What you do, in order.** (a) Say "recording" and switch Recording ON. (b) In the film profile show two pages only, the page area without tab strip, address bar or profile menu: HubSpot Contacts with the search `take-<run name>` (the owner tells you the run name), and the receiver Gmail list of label `demo`. (c) Read both counts aloud to the owner. (d) Say "filmed", switch Recording OFF, check that the recording tool says stopped, and leave the screen to the owner.
4. **Say "stop" at once, switch Recording OFF and touch nothing else** if you see: any long random-looking text, a key or password, a mail address other than the three made-up ones (`take-<run name>-a@example.com`, `-b@`, `-c@`), a file path, or the owner asks you to. Do not describe what you saw out loud near a microphone.
5. **Not yours.** The owner checks the counts, cancels keys, deletes contacts and mails, and posts. The editor cuts the video; the post checker checks the post. You are none of them.
6. **If anything is unclear:** stop, switch Recording OFF, and ask the owner; the owner asks the lead.
End of the film person page (same for sheet A and sheet B).
