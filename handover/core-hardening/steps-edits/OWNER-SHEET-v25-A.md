Dated Mon 16 Nov 2026. Do not use any older sheet (v4 to v24). Text between backticks is typed without the backticks. Text in angle brackets `<...>` is a value you read from the numbers message (section 3); you replace it and never type the brackets. ## 1. What happens, and the words
**The run in short.** You and the **lead** type to each other in a chat; you make a paid key, run a test program once while a **film person** films, cancel the key with **the card** (a one-page printed checklist, section 2), then delete and revoke what the lead lists. You also need two other humans: a film person and a post checker (5.2). To start, do 5.1; it ends with how to ask for the numbers message.

**The words** (each explained once, before it is used; a word keeps one meaning).
- **UTC** = world clock time. **IST** = UTC + 5:30; your phone alarms use IST. **UNVERIFIED** = nobody has run or seen it; if what you see differs, stop (STOP RULE below), except **tolerated** items (each step marks them; only button, menu and page-path wording): describe what you saw and go on. A difference in a number, name, count, account, time or key state is never tolerated.
- **Lead** = the AI session (an AI system, not a person) in the chat where you got this sheet; that chat is the **message place**: a message from anywhere else is not accepted. The lead sends the **numbers message** (every value for this run, sample in section 3); you check it by hand, type a yes and wait for "go". A **run name** is 4 to 12 lowercase letters and digits, starting with a letter, never used before. The **other channel** (card words) is named in the numbers message; none named: no run.
- **WSL** = the Ubuntu window inside Windows (Start menu, type Ubuntu, Enter). The **test terminal** and **read terminal** (card words) are two WSL windows; the read terminal is for `date -u`, `ls -l`, `clear` and the grep of 5.7 only; keep it open from 5.1.
- **Send** = type in the message place and press Enter. **Describe** = send which step you are at, what kind of line you see and plain numbers. Never copy, type or photograph a key, an app password, any long random-looking text or a real person's mail address; no screenshots. **Set phrase** = words you send exactly (list at the end of section 2).
- **Clean-up message** = the lead's message after the run (8.2) with the lists to delete and revoke.
- **Model key** = a key to the paid model's account; it lets a program spend money; it is **alive** from the create press until card step 5 is done. **Model account** = the paid model company's account where you pay and make keys; open it only from your own bookmark or an address you type. The **test program** is the program you start by hand: it sends three made-up people through **n8n** (a ready-made tool that connects services) into **HubSpot** (a ready-made online customer-record service; here a free test account), making one **contact** (customer record) and one mail per person. The **film person** is a human who alone drives and films the browser. **Older key** = a paid-model key you saved earlier, if the numbers message names one. **Valid key** = a key the key list shows as usable. **Cancelled word** = the state word of a cancelled key, given in the numbers message (none: no run).
- **Film pause** = the wait while the film person films; it starts at the **film cue** (a printed line copied in the numbers message, or minutes after you sent "run started"). **Recording OFF** = every screen recording or sharing is off. **Recording box** = the page area of the film profile's browser window (no tab strip, address bar or profile menu). **Privacy switches** = Windows Clipboard history, Sync across your devices, Recall. **Film profile** = the Chrome profile `demo-take` (5.5). The numbers message names the film person, the **editor** (crops and cuts the film) and the **post checker** (compares your post with the sentences); you are none of them.
- **Demo mail** = a mail in the receiver Gmail under label `demo` that R7 allows you to delete. **Throwaway accounts** = the receiver Gmail (gets the demo mails), the sender Gmail (sends them) and the HubSpot test account you made earlier, never your real mailbox. **App passwords** are Gmail passwords made for one application; **HubSpot app secrets** are the secret values of the HubSpot private apps the flows use.
- **Made-up people** = person A, B and C, with addresses `take-<run name>-a@example.com` (`-b@`, `-c@`); the **prep contact** = one extra made-up contact made for practice (the clean-up message lists it). **Key file** = the file in WSL holding the model key. **Model program** = the lead's software that asks the paid model with your key; you never edit its settings file or the test program's.
- **Money.** **Prepaid** = you pay first and calls draw the amount down; **auto-reload** = the account tops itself up when low (must be off). **Balance** = the whole prepaid amount: the most you can lose. **Call ceiling** and **expected total**: see check 1 of section 3. **Last-resort step** = one action in the model account that only stops spending, named in the numbers message and in your yes.
- **Times** (in the numbers message). **Run day** = the one day of the run, as in `Wed 18 Nov 2026`. **Deadline** = Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST: after it nothing may spend. **Earliest key time**: before it you do not press create. **Latest start** = the time after which you do not press Enter on the start line (7.3). **Give-up minutes** = how long the test program waits for `end` before it gives up. **Alarm time** and **cancel-by time** = when the run should be over, and the backup; **silence minutes** = how long the test terminal may print nothing before you treat it as stuck. **FREEZE** = type and close nothing more in the test terminal (except `n` at "Type y to start"); in the read terminal run only `date -u` and `ls -l "<path from the message>"`; in the browser do only the card and the deleting R7 allows; describe; wait for the lead's word "continue". If a key was alive the run ends after the card: no word resumes it. While a key is alive the card comes first, always.

**STOP RULE.** If at any moment you do not know what to do, or what you see differs from this sheet or the numbers message: (1) if a key is alive, do the card now; (2) then FREEZE and describe to the lead. A paid key left alive costs money, so doing nothing is never safe while a key is alive. If two rules disagree: the card first, then R1, then R10, then the rest.

(d) After you sent "model key cancelled" or "no key was made": only the app passwords, HubSpot apps and contacts the clean-up message names, in the throwaway accounts the numbers message names (8.2 to 8.6). (e) The last-resort step in the blank, only as "If it fails" says. Any other key or password, or a key you did not make: describe its name (if the name is a long random text, describe only that); cancel nothing. What the cancel button does is UNVERIFIED (it may not be undoable, and may not stop calls at once): treat it as final and prove it.

1. If Recording is ON: switch it OFF yourself at once with the tool the numbers message names; then go on and describe to the lead that the raw recording must be checked.
2. In your normal profile open the key list at the address in the blanks. Jot on paper how many keys it shows (N, if not already jotted) and how many keys are yours (K: the keys of list (a), normally 1; 2 if you pressed create twice). For the older key (list (b)), N is the count now and K is 1.

4. Proof: reload the page. Each of your keys shows the cancelled word, and shows it again after one more reload (yes ___ no ___); or its row is gone and the list shows exactly N minus K keys (yes ___ no ___; leave the page's filters as they were). Anything else: press cancel again and reload; after two tries see "If it fails".
5. Send exactly: model key cancelled. Only now may you do anything else.

**After the card (not part of it):** if 7.4 never happened, send "none" for the time in 8.1. If you had typed `end`, go on at 7.9. If you had not, type nothing in the test terminal and describe to the lead that the run is over without `end`; go on at 8.1 once the lead has answered or the give-up minutes have passed.

**If it fails.** The key list page does not load: retry for 2 minutes, then open the same page on your phone (UNVERIFIED), send "cancel failed", keep trying. The cancelled word differs after two tries: send "cancel failed". The latest start passes before you press Enter on the start line: do not start, send "time passed", and do the card if you made a key. **Last resort:** 10 minutes after "cancel failed" with no proof, send it again on the other channel. If nobody answers within 10 more minutes, do the last-resort step in the blank yourself, only in the model account, nothing else; no message delays it. For the older key, steps 2 to 4 apply, you send no "model key cancelled", and the proof counts as in step 4 (the cancelled word of the blanks, or the row gone and N minus 1 keys); a failure there is a stop and no key exists yet.
