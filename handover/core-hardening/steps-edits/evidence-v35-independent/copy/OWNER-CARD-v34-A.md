### THE CARD, sheet A (print the copy OWNER-CARD-v34-A.md; no printer: copy it by hand; fill the blanks before you press create of the run key)
**The five steps (at once, in this order, whenever this sheet says "do the card"):**
1. If Recording is ON: switch it OFF yourself at once with the tool the numbers message names; then describe to the lead that the raw recording must be checked.
2. In your normal profile open the key list at the address in the blanks. Write on the card how many rows it shows now (own key or older key blank) and K = how many keys you cancel now (your keys of L1, normally 1, 2 if you pressed create twice; 1 for the older key).
3. For each of your keys: press the cancel button; if a box asks, press the confirmation word. A button or box word that differs from the blank but plainly cancels only this one key (not the account, not other keys): describe it, press it; step 4 is the proof. Any other difference: press nothing, go to "If it fails". Right after the press run `date -u` in the read terminal and jot the time.
4. Proof: reload the page. Each of your keys shows the cancelled word, and shows it again after one more reload (yes ___ no ___); or its row is gone and the list shows exactly the rows you wrote minus K (yes ___ no ___; leave the page's filters as they were). Anything else: press cancel again on the same named row and reload; after two tries: "If it fails".
5. Send exactly: model key cancelled (not for a look key). Only now may you do anything else. It does not mean no valid key remains: the older key may still spend until it is cancelled (6.7 or 7.11, L2).
The look key (5.9) is the one key whose words are not on this card yet: do its steps 2 to 4 as 5.9 says.

**Danger signs (labels W1 to W9; the only reasons to do the card; any one, at once; W1 to W8 only while a key is alive):** W1 you typed `end`; W2 the run is stopped, refused or called off by anyone, or you typed `n`; W3 you do not know what to do, or a number, name, count, account, time or key state differs from this sheet or the numbers message, or the film person says "stop"; W4 the second or third phone alarm rings (after you proved the cancel: dismiss it, do nothing else); the fourth alarm is not a danger sign of the run key: it means 7.11 (the older key) now; W5 the first phone alarm rings (it rings at the latest start) and you have not pressed Enter on the start line: do not start (press Enter at least 5 minutes before the latest start; from 5 minutes before it do not press Enter, send "time passed" and do the card at once; if you had pressed it: dismiss alarm 1, do nothing); W6 the test terminal shows a shell prompt before you typed `end`; W7 the text of a model key appeared on your screen, except the key's own one-time display at 5.9 and 6.3 and the paste at 6.4; W8 a step says "do the card"; W9 the text of the older key appeared on your screen, key alive or not (your own key first if one is alive, then steps 2 to 4 for the older key, list L2). No word of the lead resumes a run whose key was alive: a new try needs a new numbers message, run name, key file, key and yes.
**Not danger signs (no card, no FREEZE):** a lead message "wait" or a slow lead (6.6: keep waiting; the latest start still holds); the y question; the wait for the film cue; the film pause; the wait for `end`; button or menu wording that differs but means the same.

**What you may cancel or revoke (the only list; its items are labelled L1 to L5):** L1 your keys made with the create button (normally 1, plus the look key): named `run-<run name>`, `look-<run name>` or the misspelled name you typed, created at or after the key-list time of that key (the run key: the card blank; the look key: its own time jotted at 5.9; not yet jotted: its create time; the key list may show its own time zone, the blanks hold both). Another key created at or after that time: describe its name, cancel nothing. L2 the older key named in the numbers message, only if its name and creation date equal the blanks; this cancel cannot be undone. If its text appeared on your screen: cancel it at once (the 7-day rule of 5.1 does not apply to that). After the deadline (Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST): the older key (its name and creation date still equal the blanks) or a key of L1 that you find valid is cancelled at once by steps 2 to 4. By the **backstop time** (Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST) the older key (name and creation date equal the blanks) and every key of L1 you made are cancelled by steps 2 to 4, even if a run was in progress (no run is planned on that day); any other valid key: describe its name, cancel nothing. The planned cancel: at 6.7, after 6.6 passed (new key saved, size checked), and only if the 7-day rule of 5.1 holds (read the older key's last-used time again right before the cancel; the list may show a use late: any use after your first reading means no cancel, describe it) (else describe it: no run). If the run ends before 6.7 the older key is still valid: you cancel it at 7.11 by the older-key time of the message on the run day, even if the lead is silent (no lead message is needed or accepted for it). L3 an app password or HubSpot app secret whose text appeared on your screen: revoke it at once (8.3, 8.4); the entry is the one with the exact name the message gives; no name known: describe, revoke nothing. L4 After you sent "model key cancelled" or "no key was made": only what the clean-up message names, in the throwaway accounts the numbers message names (8.2 to 8.6). L5 The last-resort step in the blanks, only as "If it fails" says. Any other key or password: describe its name (a long random text: describe only that); cancel nothing. The cancel button is UNVERIFIED: treat it as final and prove it.

**Blanks, filled before you press create of the run key (the three words at 5.9; the older key's last-used time, the cancel helper and the helper date come from you; the rest from the numbers message; every time written as UTC = IST):** key list address ______ | run name ______ | cancel button word ______ | confirmation box word (the button that really cancels, not one that only closes the box) ______ | cancelled word ______ | other channel ______ | backup person (the one person you call when a cancel fails) ______ | last-resort step and whether it can be undone ______ | older key name, creation date and last-used time ______ | key list time zone ______ | latest start (alarm 1) ______ = ______ | alarm time (alarm 2) ______ = ______ | cancel-by time (alarm 3) ______ = ______ | older-key time on the run day (alarm 4) ______ = ______ | deadline ______ = ______ | cancel helper (never the film person, editor, post checker or backup person of the numbers message): a person who may cancel only the keys named on the helper's paper (at most three: the run key `run-<run name>`, the look key `look-<run name>` and the older key with its creation date) in the model account if you cannot, with a cancel-only role (none, or the account offers no such role: write "none") ______ | helper phone number, first time, second time (helper page, item 1) and date page and paper given ______. **Filled after you press create (6.3):** create time (UTC) ______ | key-list time of my key (the key list's own zone) ______. **Filled in step 2:** rows now, own key ______ | rows now, older key ______.

**If it fails.** The key list does not load: retry for 2 minutes, then use your phone (UNVERIFIED; 5.3 tried it), send "cancel failed", keep trying. The cancelled word differs after two tries: send "cancel failed". The latest start is less than 5 minutes away and you have not pressed Enter on the start line: do not start, send "time passed", and do the card if you made a key. **Last resort:** 10 minutes after "cancel failed" with no proof, send it again on the other channel and call the backup person (only that one person); if nobody answers within 10 more minutes, do the last-resort step yourself, only in the model account. In those up to 22 minutes (2 of retry, then 10 and 10) the key can spend up to the whole balance (USD 189.00 in the sample). **Near the deadline you do not wait:** once a deadline reminder of 5.8 has rung, or after the deadline, do the last-resort step at once after your first "cancel failed" (retry 2 minutes, phone fallback, send "cancel failed", then the step). The waiting chain would end at Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:31 UTC = 00:01 IST when the cancel starts at the Mon 23 Nov reminder (Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:09 UTC = 23:39 IST), and at 18:41 UTC = 00:11 IST when it starts at the backstop time: both after the deadline. If your cancel helper (page OWNER-HELPER-v34.md; appendix part 10) cannot cancel either, the helper does the last-resort step on its paper (none: only yours is left); call the backup person at once. For the older key, steps 2 to 4 apply and you send no "model key cancelled"; proof failing after two tries: send "cancel failed", do the card for your own key, no run; the last resort applies (the older key may still spend).

**After the card (not part of it):** if 7.4 never happened, send "none" for the time in 8.1. If you had typed `end`, proceed at 7.9; if not, type nothing in the test terminal, describe that the run is over without `end`, and go on once the lead has answered or the give-up minutes have passed. Never pressed create of the run key and the run is over (decline, time passed, a check failed after "go"): send "no key was made". **Every way the run ends (finished, "I decline", "time passed", "key save failed, cancelled, new run name <name>", "cancel failed", FREEZE, no `end`) passes 7.11 before 8.1.** When no key is alive and the run is over, put the Privacy switches back as the first sentence of 8.7 says.

**Words on this card.** **Alive** = from the moment you press the create button of the key list until step 5 is done (a look key: until step 4 is done). **Latest start** = the time in the blanks (alarm 1): press Enter on the start line at least 5 minutes before it (so "restarted", 6.6, must have come by then); from 5 minutes before it, do not press Enter (send "time passed" and do the card at once). **Phone alarm** = one of the four alarms of 6.2: alarm 1 at the latest start, alarm 2 at the alarm time, alarm 3 at the cancel-by time, alarm 4 at the older-key time (all times in the blanks); the two **deadline reminders** (5.8: Sun 22 Nov 2026 18:09 UTC = 23:39 IST and Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:09 UTC = 23:39 IST) are a fifth and a sixth phone alarm, set at 5.8. **Backstop time** = Sun 22 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST or Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST (after the reminder that rang), the last time by which the older key (L2) and every key of L1 must be cancelled; the cancel-by time (alarm 3) belongs to the run day. **Read terminal** = a second Ubuntu window where you only type `date -u`, `ls -l`, `clear` and the grep of 5.7. **Normal profile** = the Chrome profile you always use. **Recording** = any screen recording or sharing.

**Set phrases (send each exactly, `<...>` filled; anything else is a description; "none" only at 8.1):** "I decline"; "send the numbers message for run <run name>"; "I cancel the older key <its name>, nothing else I know uses it"; "I cancel the older key <its name> now, the yes covers it"; "model key cancelled"; "no key was made"; "time passed"; "cancel failed"; "key save failed, cancelled, new run name <name>"; "run started"; "contacts deleted <number>"; "app passwords revoked"; "HubSpot apps revoked"; "demo mails deleted <number>"; "accepted"; "posted"; "got a message that says <in words>, is it yours" (R8, other channel); the yes (5.8). Spoken only to the film person: "stop".
