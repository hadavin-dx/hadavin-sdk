# OWNER-DAY-SHEET: the page for the take day (saved in the same folder as the owner sheet)

This page is for the day of the take. It repeats nothing new: the card, the alarms, the last-day plan and the table of typed words are the same text as in step 22 item 3 of the owner sheet (key steps B to D and G), and the full reasons are in `OWNER-APPENDIX-K.md`. Only messages that you type yourself, as a new message in your chat with the assistant, count.

## The card
The one order (the card; read it first). Do these seven things in this order and never a later one before an earlier one:
1. You: make sure that you have confirmed your last take with `yes, no more takes` (key step D.3), then revoke the secret key for the model (key step I) and write `key revoked` (on Mon 23 Nov 2026, if you have not typed `yes, no more takes` by 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST, revoke the key at that time anyway and write `key gone, no more decisions` instead of both). Make new keys first only if key step H says so and at least 30 minutes remain. Normally you do this right after your last take that you accept.
2. You: do your items 2 and 3, the quick web revokes, each only after the identification check of step 22 item 3 (item 2: revoke both app passwords; item 3: delete both HubSpot apps; touch nothing that you are not sure about).
3. You: shred the copy of the key in the take folder and the other copies of the old key on your own list (key step G, parts (a) and (b)) and write `shred done key copies`.
4. You: do your item 4 (save the screenshots) and your item 5, the retire check.
5. You: do your item 6 (shred its two files) and shred the other secret files on your own list (key step G, parts (c) and (d)), and write `shred done other files`.
6. You: read the list of what the assistant will remove (the full paths and the container's name) and, if nothing in it is a file that you want to keep, write `start clean-up`.
7. Then the assistant does its item 1 (the clean-up). It never starts while a copy of the key exists and the key is not revoked.
Last day, Mon 23 Nov 2026: the 10 minutes between 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST and 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST go in this order: card step 1 (the key), then card step 2 (the quick web revokes), then card step 3 (the shreds). What you have not finished by 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST you finish by Tue 24 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST (key step D.5).
Key step J (other keys) is not one of the seven: do it at any time after card step 1; it holds nothing up.

## Your three alarms
Your alarms. The assistant has no timer, and nobody else reminds you on Mon 23 Nov 2026. Set three alarms on your own phone or computer. Alarm 1: Mon 23 Nov 2026 17:49 UTC = 23:19 IST, named `revoke the secret key for the model`. Alarm 2: a repeating alarm in the evening of every day from the day of your first take until Mon 23 Nov 2026 (the time of day is yours, in your own local time), named `is the secret key for the model still alive?`. Alarm 3: Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:09 UTC = 23:39 IST, named `10 minutes left: revoke the secret key for the model now`. When alarm 1 rings: if a session with the assistant is open and it asks you its two questions (key step F), answer them, but do not wait for them; if no session is open or it does not ask, go straight to key step I. Make no new key now unless at least 30 minutes remain after you have answered (key step H); alarm 1 gives exactly 30 minutes, so on the last day you normally make none. If a take is running when alarm 1 rings, the take may finish, but you do key step I by 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST at the latest, whether the take has finished or not (key step K). When alarm 3 rings and the key is not yet revoked: if no take is running, stop everything else and do key step I now; if a take is running, let it finish (key step K), then revoke the key at once, and in every case by 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST. Write `key revoked` when it is done. If you have already revoked the key, the alarms cost you nothing. When alarm 2 rings and the key is not yet revoked: if you have not chosen a route, choose one now (key step D.1); if you have chosen and your last take is done, do key step D.4 for your route (route A: confirm and follow the card; route B: watch the cut).

## The last day
D.5. The last time to revoke, and the last day. If you have not yet typed `yes, no more takes` by Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST, you revoke the key at that time anyway (key step I) and then write `key gone, no more decisions`: that one message tells the assistant that the key is gone, that there are no more takes and (in route B) that the cut is final. A `key revoked` is only a report, at any time, also after 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST: the assistant never answers it with "Do you also want no more takes? Then write `key gone, no more decisions`" and starts nothing that cannot be undone until you do. Between 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST and 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST there are only 10 minutes. Use them in this order: card step 1 (the key), then card step 2 (the app passwords and the HubSpot apps: quick clicks on websites that do not need the key for the model), and only then card step 3 (the shred of the key copies, which protects nothing once the key is dead). What you have not finished by 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST you finish yourself by Tue 24 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST: you act, and nobody does an owner item for you. The assistant lists each open item in the handoff with that date and asks you at your next session (key step G.2). Until you finish them, the HubSpot apps and the app passwords stay alive; they reach only the made-up data of your test accounts. A confirmation cannot be withdrawn once the key is revoked. The assistant says in its next message how it treated your words.

## When the clean-up may start
G.2. When you are done with parts (a) and (b), write `shred done key copies`; when you are done with parts (c) and (d), write `shred done other files`. `shred done key copies` means that EVERY file of (a) and (b) is shredded, and `shred done other files` that EVERY file of (c) and (d) is shredded, each by this rule (so `shred done key copies` and `shred done other files` together mean every file of (a), (b), (c) and (d)). If even one is not, write `shred not done key copies` or `shred not done other files`, the last part of the name of each file that is left (never its content) and the reason. If you write neither, the assistant treats it as `shred not done key copies` or `shred not done other files`. The assistant never opens the files. Shredding does not revoke the key, and it is not reliable on every disk, so it does not replace key step I. Item 1 starts only when ALL of these are true, each as a message that you typed yourself: a confirmation (`yes, no more takes` or `key gone, no more decisions`; key step D.3); `key revoked` (or `key gone, no more decisions`, which counts as `key revoked`; or `key not revoked` and the reason, but item 1 never starts while a copy of the key exists and the key is not revoked); `shred done key copies` (or `shred not done key copies` and the reason); your items 2, 3, 4 and 5 done; `shred done other files` (or `shred not done other files` and the reason); you have read the list of what the assistant will remove; and `start clean-up`. If by 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST (the assistant has no timer: it reads the time shown on your last message in the chat, and a message with no time shown counts as late) you have not written ALL of these, the assistant does not start item 1 in that session; it lists in the handoff every item that is left, with the date Tue 24 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST (or a later date that you give) as the date to finish, and asks you at your next session. Item 1 may start in a later session, but still only when ALL of these are true. The assistant never does an owner item for you (the web revokes, the shreds, the retire check). Detail: appendix K-G.
