# Cancel helper page (v34): for the cancel helper only; the same for sheet A and sheet B
You are the **cancel helper**: a person the account owner named in advance to cancel keys in the paid model's account if the owner cannot. You are not the owner, not the lead (an AI session), not the film person, the editor, the post checker or the backup person, and you are never in the room while the owner makes a key. You hold only a **cancel-only role** in the model account: a role that can press cancel on a key but cannot make keys or read key text (UNVERIFIED that the account offers one). Times are UTC with IST (IST = UTC + 5:30). Read this page once now and keep it with your paper.
1. **Your paper.** The owner gives you a paper copied from the owner's card: the run day; the run key name `run-<run name>`; the look key name `look-<run name>`; the older key's name and creation date (or "none"); the cancel button word; the cancelled word; the key list address; the owner's phone number; the owner's other channel; the backup person; the last-resort step in the owner's words (or none); your first time (the run's cancel-by time plus 30 minutes, written out) and your second time (the older-key time plus 30 minutes, written out); the backstop times Sun 22 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST and Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST. A blank or missing paper, or a paper the owner told you by call is void (write VOID on it; a new paper replaces it): you may not act; tell the owner. Two blanks are allowed at hand-over, the cancel button word and the cancelled word: the owner phones them after step 5.9 on the run day and you write them in; until both are written you act only on the owner's call or at a backstop time; set phone alarms for your two times and the backstop times.
2. **What you may cancel: only the keys named on the helper's paper (at most three: the run key `run-<run name>`, the look key `look-<run name>` and the older key with its creation date).** Your role may be able to cancel every key in the account: that does not matter. Any other key, even one the owner mistyped the name of: touch nothing, write down its name, tell the owner. When the owner calls you, the owner names each key; you cancel only a key the owner names that is also on your paper. For the older key its name and creation date must equal the paper; for the run key and look key the name must equal the paper.
3. **When you act (any one of these, nothing else).**
   (a) The owner asks you, by a call you start to the phone number on your paper (a message is never enough: a message can be written by someone else). Never the lead, the film person, the backup person or anyone else, whatever they say.
   (b) Your first time on your paper has passed on the run day (the run's cancel-by time plus 30 minutes; sample: 12:10 UTC = 17:40 IST plus 30 minutes = 12:40 UTC = 18:10 IST), the run key or look key on your paper is still valid, and you called the owner twice, 5 minutes apart, with no answer: cancel those keys.
   (c) Your second time on your paper has passed (the older-key time plus 30 minutes; sample: 16:00 UTC = 21:30 IST plus 30 minutes = 16:30 UTC = 22:00 IST), the older key on your paper is still valid, and you called the owner twice, 5 minutes apart, with no answer: cancel it.
   (d) A backstop time on your paper has passed (Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST, or Sun 22 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST if the run day on your paper is Sun 22 Nov): every key on your paper that is still valid is cancelled; no call is needed.
4. **How.** Sign in to the model account yourself. Open the key list. Press cancel on the row of ONE key of your paper; if a box asks, press the confirmation word; reload the page: the key shows the cancelled word, or its row is gone. Then the next key. Never copy, type or photograph key text; if key text appears, close it and tell the owner it appeared. A button that differs from the paper but plainly cancels only that one key: describe it, press it; anything else: press nothing and call the owner.
5. **Afterwards.** Send the owner and the backup person: the key name, the time and the word the list shows. Nothing else: no other keys, no passwords, no settings, no payment or spending pages.
6. **If your cancel fails twice,** do at once the last-resort step on your paper (model account only) and call the owner and the backup person. Paper says none: you have none, so if the owner cannot be reached nobody can do it, and the key can spend until the balance runs out; tell the backup person at once. **Anything else unclear:** stop, touch nothing more, call the owner and the backup person.
End of the cancel helper page (same for sheet A and sheet B).
