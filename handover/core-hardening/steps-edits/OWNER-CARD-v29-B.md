### THE CARD, sheet B (print the copy OWNER-CARD-v29-B.md; no printer: copy it by hand; fill the blanks before you press create of the run key)
**The five steps (at once, in this order, whenever this sheet says "do the card"):**
1. If Recording is ON: switch it OFF yourself at once with the tool the numbers message names; then describe to the lead that the raw recording must be checked.
2. In your normal profile open the key list at the address in the blanks. Write on the card how many rows it shows now (own key or older key blank) and K = how many keys you cancel now (your keys of L1, normally 1, 2 if you pressed create twice; 1 for the older key).
3. For each of your keys: press the cancel button; if a box asks, press the confirmation word. A button or box word that differs from the blank but plainly cancels only this one key (not the account, not other keys): describe it, press it; step 4 is the proof. Any other difference: press nothing, go to "If it fails". Right after the press run `date -u` in the read terminal and jot the time.
4. Proof: reload the page. Each of your keys shows the cancelled word, and shows it again after one more reload (yes ___ no ___); or its row is gone and the list shows exactly the rows you wrote minus K (yes ___ no ___; leave the page's filters as they were). Anything else: press cancel again and reload; after two tries: "If it fails".
5. Send exactly: model key cancelled (not for a look key). Only now may you do anything else. It does not mean no valid key remains: the older key may still spend until 7.11 (L2).
The look key (5.9) is the one key whose words are not on this card yet: do its steps 2 to 4 as 5.9 says.

**Danger signs (the only reasons to do the card; any one, at once; W1 to W8 only while a key is alive):** W1 you typed `end`; W2 the run is stopped, refused or called off by anyone, or you typed `n`; W3 you do not know what to do, or a number, name, count, account, time or key state differs from this sheet or the numbers message; W4 the second or third phone alarm rings (after you proved the cancel: dismiss it, do nothing else); the fourth alarm is not a danger sign of the run key: it means 7.11 (the older key) now; W5 the first phone alarm rings or the latest start has passed and you have not pressed Enter on the start line (if you had pressed it: dismiss alarm 1, do nothing); W6 the test terminal shows a shell prompt before you typed `end`; W7 the text of a model key appeared on your screen, except the key's own one-time display at 5.9 and 6.3 and the paste at 6.4; W8 a step says "do the card"; W9 the text of the older key appeared on your screen, key alive or not (your own key first if one is alive, then steps 2 to 4 for the older key, list L2). No word of the lead resumes a run whose key was alive: a new try needs a new numbers message, run name, key file, key and yes.
**Not danger signs (no card, no FREEZE):** a lead message "wait" or a slow lead (6.6: keep waiting; the latest start still holds); the y question; the wait for the film cue; the film pause; the wait for `end`; button or menu wording that differs but means the same.

**What you may cancel or revoke (the only list):** L1 your keys made with the create button (normally 1, plus the look key): named `run-<run name>`, `look-<run name>` or the misspelled name you typed, created at or after the key-list time of that key (the run key: the card blank; the look key: its own time jotted at 5.9; not yet jotted: its create time; the key list may show its own time zone, the blanks hold both). Another key created at or after that time: describe its name, cancel nothing. L2 the older key named in the numbers message, only if its name and creation date equal the blanks; this cancel cannot be undone. If its text appeared on your screen: cancel it at once. The planned cancel: at 7.11, always, by the older-key time of the message on the run day, even if the lead is silent. Until then the older key stays valid and can spend. L3 an app password or HubSpot app secret whose text appeared on your screen: revoke it at once (8.3, 8.4); the entry is the one with the exact name the message gives; no name known: describe, revoke nothing. L4 After you sent "model key cancelled" or "no key was made": only what the clean-up message names, in the throwaway accounts the numbers message names (8.2 to 8.6). L5 The last-resort step in the blanks, only as "If it fails" says. Any other key or password: describe its name (a long random text: describe only that); cancel nothing. The cancel button is UNVERIFIED: treat it as final and prove it.

**If it fails.** The key list does not load: retry for 2 minutes, then use your phone (UNVERIFIED; 5.3 tried it), send "cancel failed", keep trying. The cancelled word differs after two tries: send "cancel failed". The latest start passes before you press Enter on the start line: do not start, send "time passed", and do the card if you made a key. **Last resort:** 10 minutes after "cancel failed" with no proof, send it again on the other channel and call the backup person (the person you asked about the step before the yes); if nobody answers within 10 more minutes, do the last-resort step yourself, only in the model account. In those up to 20 minutes the key can spend up to the whole balance (USD 189.00 in the sample). For the older key, steps 2 to 4 apply and you send no "model key cancelled"; proof failing after two tries: send "cancel failed"; the last resort applies (the older key may still spend).

**After the card (not part of it):** if 7.4 never happened, send "none" for the time in 8.1. If you had typed `end`, proceed at 7.9; if not, type nothing in the test terminal, describe that the run is over without `end`, and go on once the lead has answered or the give-up minutes have passed. Never pressed create of the run key and the run is over (decline, time passed, a check failed after "go"): send "no key was made". **Every way the run ends (finished, "I decline", "time passed", "key save failed, cancelled, new run name <name>", "cancel failed", FREEZE, no `end`) passes 7.11 before 8.1.** When no key is alive and the run is over, put the Privacy switches back as the first sentence of 8.7 says.
