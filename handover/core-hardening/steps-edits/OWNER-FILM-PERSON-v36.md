# Film person page (v36): for the film person only; the same for sheet A and sheet B
You are the **film person**: a human helper who films two browser pages for the account owner (the **editor** is a different person who cuts the video afterwards). You are not the owner and you never hold the owner's keys or passwords. The owner reads the long sheet; you read only this page. Times are UTC with IST (IST = UTC + 5:30).
1. **What you may touch.** Only the browser window of the Chrome profile `demo-take` (the film profile) and the screen recording tool the owner names. Never the test terminal (a black Ubuntu window), never the model account, never another profile, never a key, never a password, never a file manager, a second terminal window or the clipboard (no copy and paste): the owner's key is alive during your whole filming.
2. **When.** You start only when the owner calls you in and says aloud "film pause now" (those are your start words; you cannot see the test terminal and need not). The film pause lasts as long as the owner tells you (the sample says 10 minutes). Before that, wait where the owner puts you; the owner may be alone at a screen for a while: that is normal.
3. **What you do, in order.** (a) Look at the recording tool: its microphone and sound must be OFF; if you cannot see that or switch them off, stop and call the owner. Then say "recording" and switch Recording ON. (b) In the film profile show two pages only, the page area without tab strip, address bar or profile menu. How (UNVERIFIED, tolerated): press F11 in the browser window (full screen), or set the recording tool to record only the page area; look at its preview before you record: if any of the three still shows, switch to the other way; if that fails too, stop and call the owner. The two pages are: HubSpot Contacts with the search `take-<run name>` (the owner tells you the run name), and the receiver Gmail list of label `demo`. (c) Read both counts aloud to the owner. (d) Say "filmed", switch Recording OFF, check that the recording tool says stopped, and leave the screen to the owner. The recording file goes where the owner says; you keep no copy.
4. **Say "stop" at once, switch Recording OFF and touch nothing else** if you see: any long random-looking text, a key or password, a mail address that is neither one of the three made-up ones (`take-<run name>-a@example.com`, `-b@`, `-c@`) nor in the From column of the mail list (that column always shows the sender's throwaway address: do not stop for it, the editor cuts it), a number that identifies an account (an account id or contact id), text with its middle hidden (a masked account), a tab title or profile name that shows a mail address, a file path, or the owner asks you to. Your "stop" tells the owner to cancel the key at once. Say only "stop" aloud, and say what you saw only after Recording is OFF.
5. **Not yours.** The owner checks the counts, cancels keys, deletes contacts and mails, and posts. The editor cuts the video; the post checker checks the post. You are none of them.
6. **If anything is unclear:** stop, switch Recording OFF, and ask the owner; the owner asks the lead.
End of the film person page (same for sheet A and sheet B).
