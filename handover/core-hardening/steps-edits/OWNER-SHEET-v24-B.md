## 1. What happens, and the words
**The run in ten lines.** (1) You choose a **run name** (4 to 12 lowercase letters and digits, starting with a letter, never used before; rules in 5.1) and **tell** (type in the chat where you got this sheet) the **lead** (the AI session in that chat: an AI system, not a person). (2) The lead sends the **numbers message** (one message with every value for this run, sample in section 3); you check the numbers by hand and type a yes. (3) The lead says "go". (4) You make a fresh **model key** (a key to the paid model's account; it lets a program spend money) and save it in a file the lead's program reads; from then the key is **alive**, which means it can spend money until you cancel it. (5) You start the **test program** (the program you start by hand) and answer it: `y`, a number it shows, and later `end`. (6) The test program sends three made-up people into **n8n** (a ready-made tool that connects services without writing code); n8n makes one **contact** (a customer record) per person in **HubSpot** (a ready-made online service for customer records; here a free test account) and one mail per person, and asks the paid model at most once per person. (7) A **film person** (a human who alone drives and films the browser) films the contact list and the mail list. (8) You type `end`, then cancel the key with **the card** (the one-page checklist of section 2, which you print) and prove it. (9) The lead tells you what to delete and revoke; you do it by hand. (10) The film is checked, and a sentence is posted if everything agrees. If you ever do not know what to do: the STOP RULE at the end of this section.

**The words** (each explained once, before it is used; a word means the same thing everywhere).
