### THE CARD, sheet A (print the copy OWNER-CARD-v36-A.md; no printer: copy it by hand; fill the blanks before you press create of the run key)
**The five steps (at once, in this order, whenever this sheet says "do the card"):**
1. If Recording is ON: switch it OFF yourself at once with the tool the numbers message names; then describe to the lead that the raw recording must be checked.
2. In your normal profile open the key list at the address in the blanks. Write on the card how many rows it shows now (own key or older key blank) and K = how many keys you cancel now (your keys of L1, normally 1, 2 if you pressed create twice; 1 for the older key).
3. For each of your keys: press the cancel button; if a box asks, press the confirmation word. A button or box word that differs from the blank but plainly cancels only this one key (not the account, not other keys): describe it, press it; step 4 is the proof. Any other difference: press nothing, go to "If it fails". Right after the press run `date -u` in the read terminal and jot the time.
4. Proof: reload the page. Each of your keys shows the cancelled word, and shows it again after one more reload (yes ___ no ___); or its row is gone and the list shows exactly the rows you wrote minus K (yes ___ no ___; leave the page's filters as they were). Anything else: press cancel again on the same named row and reload; after two tries: "If it fails".
5. Send exactly: model key cancelled (not for a look key). Only now may you do anything else. It does not mean no valid key remains: the older key may still spend until it is cancelled (6.7 or 7.11, L2).
The look key (5.9) is the one key whose words are not on this card yet: do its steps 2 to 4 as 5.9 says.

**Planned card moments (labels P1 and P2; normal steps, not danger signs):** P1 you typed `end` (7.8); P2 you typed `n` (7.4, R5).
**Danger signs (labels W3 to W9; this card has no W1 or W2, nothing is missing; the only other reasons to do the card; any one, at once; W3 to W8 only while a key is alive):**
W3 the run is stopped, refused or called off by anyone, or you do not know what to do, or a number, name, count, account, time or key state differs from this sheet or the numbers message, or the film person says "stop";
W4 the second or third phone alarm rings (after you proved the cancel: dismiss it, do nothing else); the fourth alarm is not a danger sign of the run key: it means 7.11 (the older key) now;
W5 the Enter-by time (the latest start minus 5 minutes) has passed and you have not pressed Enter on the start line: do not start, send "time passed" and do the card at once (alarm 1 at the latest start repeats it; if you pressed Enter in time: dismiss alarm 1, do nothing);
W6 the test terminal shows a shell prompt before you typed `end`;
W7 the text of a model key appeared on your screen, except the key's own one-time display at 5.9 and 6.3 and the paste at 6.4;
W8 a step says "do the card";
W9 the text of the older key appeared on your screen, key alive or not (your own key first if one is alive, then steps 2 to 4 for the older key, list L2).
No word of the lead resumes a run whose key was alive: a new try needs a new numbers message, run name, key file, key and yes.
**Not danger signs (no card, no FREEZE):** a lead message "wait" or a slow lead (6.6: keep waiting; the latest start still holds); the y question; the wait for the film cue; the film pause; the wait for `end`; button or menu wording that differs but means the same.

**What you may cancel or revoke (the only list; its items are labelled L1 to L5):** L1 your keys made with the create button (normally 1, plus the look key): named `run-<run name>`, `look-<run name>` or the misspelled name you typed, created at or after the key-list time of that key (the run key: the card blank; the look key: its own time jotted at 5.9; not yet jotted: its create time; the key list may show its own time zone, the blanks hold both). Another key created at or after that time: describe its name, cancel nothing.

L2 the older key named in the numbers message, only if its name and creation date equal the blanks; this cancel cannot be undone. If its text appeared on your screen: cancel it at once (the 7-day rule of 5.1 does not apply to that).

After the deadline (Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST): the older key (its name and creation date still equal the blanks) or a key of L1 that you find valid is cancelled at once by steps 2 to 4.

By the **backstop time** (Sun 22 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST or Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST; the 7-day rule does not apply to it) the older key (name and creation date equal the blanks) and every key of L1 you made are cancelled by steps 2 to 4, even if a run was in progress (no run is planned on Mon 23 Nov; a run on Sun 22 Nov is allowed only under numbers check 2); any other valid key: describe its name, cancel nothing.

The planned cancel: at 6.7, after 6.6 passed, and only if the 7-day rule of 5.1 holds (a use after your first reading: no cancel at 6.7, describe it). Otherwise you cancel it at 7.11 by the older-key time of the message on the run day, even if the lead is silent (no lead message is needed or accepted for it).



L3 an app password or HubSpot app secret whose text appeared on your screen: revoke it at once (8.3, 8.4); the entry is the one with the exact name the message gives; no name known: describe, revoke nothing.

L4 After you sent "model key cancelled" or "no key was made": only what the clean-up message names, in the throwaway accounts the numbers message names (8.2 to 8.6).

L5 The last-resort step in the blanks, only as "If it fails" says. Any other key or password: describe its name (a long random text: describe only that); cancel nothing. The cancel button is UNVERIFIED: treat it as final and prove it.

**Blanks, filled before you press create of the run key (the three words at 5.9; the older key's last-used time, the cancel helper and the helper date come from you; the rest from the numbers message; every time written as UTC = IST):** key list address ______ | run name ______ | cancel button word ______ | confirmation box word (the button that really cancels, not one that only closes the box) ______ | cancelled word ______ | other channel ______ | backup person (the one person you call when a cancel fails) ______ | last-resort step and whether it can be undone ______ | older key name, creation date and last-used time ______ | key list time zone ______ | latest start (alarm 1) ______ = ______ | alarm time (alarm 2) ______ = ______ | cancel-by time (alarm 3) ______ = ______ | older-key time on the run day (alarm 4) ______ = ______ | deadline ______ = ______ | cancel helper (never the film person, editor, post checker or backup person of the numbers message): a person who may cancel only the keys named on the helper's paper in the model account if you cannot, with a cancel-only role (write "none" if the account offers no such role or the role can do more than cancel keys) ______ | helper phone number, first time, second time (helper page, item 1) and date page and paper given ______. **Filled after you press create (6.3):** create time (UTC) ______ | key-list time of my key (the key list's own zone) ______. **Filled in step 2:** rows now, own key ______ | rows now, older key ______.

**If it fails.** The key list does not load: retry for 2 minutes, then use your phone (UNVERIFIED; 5.3 tried it), send "cancel failed", keep trying. The cancelled word differs after two tries: send "cancel failed". The Enter-by time has passed and you have not pressed Enter on the start line: do not start, send "time passed", and do the card if you made a key. **Last resort:** 10 minutes after "cancel failed" with no proof, send it again on the other channel and call the backup person (only that one person); if nobody answers within 10 more minutes, do the last-resort step yourself, only in the model account. In those up to 22 minutes (2 of retry, then 10 and 10) the key can spend up to the whole balance (USD 189.00 in the sample). **From alarm 5 on you do not wait (one rule for both days; on Sunday the waiting chain would still end before the deadline):** once alarm 5 or alarm 6 has rung, or after the deadline, do the last-resort step at once after your first "cancel failed" (retry 2 minutes, phone fallback, send "cancel failed", then the step). The waiting chain would end at Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:31 UTC = 00:01 IST (cancel started at alarm 6) or 18:41 UTC = 00:11 IST (at the backstop time): both after the deadline. If your cancel helper (page OWNER-HELPER-v36.md; appendix part 10) cannot cancel either, only your last-resort step is left (the helper never does it); call the backup person at once. For the older key, steps 2 to 4 apply and you send no "model key cancelled"; proof failing after two tries: send "cancel failed", do the card for your own key, no run; the last resort applies (the older key may still spend).

**After the card (not part of it):** if 7.4 never happened, send "none" for the time in 8.1. If you had typed `end`, proceed at 7.9; if not, type nothing in the test terminal, describe that the run is over without `end`, and go on once the lead has answered or the give-up minutes have passed. Never pressed create of the run key and the run is over (decline, time passed, a check failed after "go"): send "no key was made". **Every way the run ends (finished, "I decline", "time passed", "key save failed, cancelled, new run name <name>", "cancel failed", FREEZE, no `end`) passes 7.11 before 8.1.** When no key is alive, 7.11 is done and the run is over without section 8 (decline, "time passed", run moved or off), do the first and second parts of 8.7 at once (Privacy switches back; the helper's role removed and the paper voided by phone); otherwise do them at 8.7.

**Words on this card.** **Alive** = from the moment you press the create button of the key list until step 5 is done (a look key: until step 4 is done). **Latest start** = the time in the blanks (alarm 1). **Enter-by time** = the latest start minus 5 minutes (sample 11:25 UTC = 16:55 IST): press Enter on the start line at or before it, never after it ("restarted", 6.6, must have come by then); too late: send "time passed" and do the card at once. **Phone alarm** = one of the four alarms of 6.2: alarm 1 at the latest start, alarm 2 at the alarm time, alarm 3 at the cancel-by time, alarm 4 at the older-key time (all times in the blanks); **Alarm 5** rings Sun 22 Nov 2026 18:09 UTC = 23:39 IST and **alarm 6** Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:09 UTC = 23:39 IST; both are set at 5.8 and each means 7.11 now. **Backstop time** = ten minutes after alarm 5 (Sun 22 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST) or alarm 6 (Mon 23 Nov 2026 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST), the last time by which the older key (L2) and every key of L1 must be cancelled; the cancel-by time (alarm 3) belongs to the run day. **Read terminal** = a second Ubuntu window where you only type `date -u`, `ls -l`, `clear` and the grep of 5.7. **Normal profile** = the Chrome profile you always use. **Recording** = any screen recording or sharing.

**Set phrases (send each exactly, `<...>` filled; anything else is a description; "none" only at 8.1):** "I decline"; "send the numbers message for run <run name>"; "I cancel the older key <its name>, nothing else I know uses it"; "I cancel the older key <its name> now, the yes covers it"; "model key cancelled"; "no key was made"; "time passed"; "cancel failed"; "key save failed, cancelled, new run name <name>"; "run started"; "contacts deleted <number>"; "app passwords revoked"; "HubSpot apps revoked"; "demo mails deleted <number>"; "accepted"; "posted"; "got a message that says <in words>, is it yours" (R8, other channel); the yes (5.8). Spoken only to the film person: "stop".
