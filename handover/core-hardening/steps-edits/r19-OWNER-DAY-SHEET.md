# OWNER-DAY-SHEET: the page for the take day (saved in the same folder as the owner sheet)

This page is for the day of the take. Everything on it is the same text as in step 22 item 3 of the owner sheet (key steps B to D, G and I, the card, the alarms and the table of typed words), and the full reasons are in `OWNER-APPENDIX-K.md`. From the owner sheet you need, besides this page, only key step A (the fixed rule), key step E (what the assistant gives you), key step F (the two questions), key step H (new keys, only if you need them), key step J (other keys) and key step K (a take still running). Only messages that you type yourself, as a new message in your chat with the assistant, count.

## Words used on this page
Words in this item: a step is a numbered step of this sheet; an item is one of the numbered items 1 to 6 of step 22; a key step is one of the lettered key steps A to K; a card step is one of the seven numbered lines of the card; `the key` always means the secret key for the model that the take used (every other key is named); `the last 10 minutes` means only the span that the last-day sentence of the card names; the retire check is your item 5 (it tries to log in with the two secrets that the take used, and every login must be refused); the retirement is the whole of step 22.

What you have not finished by 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST you finish by Tue 24 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST (this date is to be confirmed by the studio lead before you rely on it) (key step D.5); the key is never one of those things: it is not deferrable (key step D.5).

## Why 18:19 and 18:29
Why these two times: 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST is the end of the spending window (from then on the model stage may not spend money). 18:19 UTC = 23:49 IST is the last time to revoke, set by the security decision of 16 Nov 2026, so that the key is dead before the window ends; the key can spend until the moment it is revoked, which is why it comes first in the last 10 minutes. The spending cap of the model stage limits the program only, not a copy of the key. Whether the company that provides the AI model can also limit this key is not checked, so until you revoke it the key can spend up to what your account at that company allows.

## The identification check (before you revoke an app password or delete a HubSpot app)
Identify first, then revoke or delete (the rule for item 2 and item 3). Before the take you wrote on paper or on your phone a list of the two app passwords and of the two HubSpot private apps, with their names and the day each was made, and for each one whether your own n8n (the one at `http://localhost:5678`) uses it (the Phase 0 note (under NOT YET CONFIRMED), point (5)). Which one counts as used by your own n8n: a HubSpot app counts as used when the HubSpot credential in your own n8n (your statement says that it has one) holds that app's secret key; an app password counts as used when the Gmail credential in your own n8n holds it; which key or password those credentials hold is not known (UNVERIFIED), so before the take you open each of them and write beside each of the four items `used` or `not used`, and where you cannot tell you write `used`; the app that step 7 calls the app for n8n (app 2) is the one most likely to be used. On the day, open the page, compare each name and date with that list yourself, and revoke or delete an item only if it is on your list and your list says that your own n8n does not use it. If a name or date is not on your list, or your list says that your own n8n uses it, or you are not sure, touch NOTHING of that kind: write `web revokes not done`, the names and dates and the reason (never a key or a password). The assistant lists each such item in the handoff with you as its owner and the date Tue 24 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST (this date is to be confirmed by the studio lead before you rely on it), and item 1 may still start (key step G.2); the retire check is not run for it (your item 6 says what to do). Deleting a private app cannot be undone (the app and its secret key are gone for good), and it breaks everything that uses it.

## The shred rule (key step G)
Key step G. The shred rule. It is ONE rule for every file that you shred in this item and in step 8 (sub-steps e and f). Parts (a) and (b) are shredded only after the key is revoked. Which files may be shredded: (a) the copy of the key in the take folder, by the path that you checked in key step E; (b) the files on YOUR OWN LIST that hold a copy of the OLD KEY for the model, a list that you wrote by hand on paper or on your phone BEFORE the take (the Phase 0 note (under NOT YET CONFIRMED), point (4)), each with its full path; (c) the two files of item 6 below, one at a time; (d) every other file on your own list: the password files and editor copies made in step 8: the password file of sub-steps a to e, any old password file, `n8n.env`, and any file whose name ends in `~` or `.save` next to them, and any other file that holds a secret you saved (such as an app password file), each by its full path from your own list. Your own list is the list of every file that holds a secret you saved; you add a file to it the moment you make it and mark it `old key` (part (b)) or `other` (part (d)); a file is in exactly one part. Nothing else. Never shred a recording, a video file, a screenshot or anything under `/mnt/c` that is not on your own list, and never a file that only the assistant named. Do not shred part (a) while a take is running (the model stage reads that copy); wait until the take has stopped. Before you run `shred -u` on a file, check all of these, in this order. (1) The path is written in full and starts with `/` (never `~` and never a dot). (2) It contains no `..`, no space, no quote sign and none of the signs `*`, `?` and `[`. (3) `realpath '<path>'` prints exactly the same path, which shows that no part of the path is a link (a link is a shortcut to another place). (4) `ls -l '<path>'` shows a line that starts with `-` (a plain file, not a folder and not a link) and that has the number `1` in its second column (the file has only one name; a file with two names would also destroy the content of the other name). (5) For part (a): the path starts with the full path of the take folder followed by `/`, so that the take folder `/x/take-1` never accepts `/x/take-10/key`. For a file of part (b) or (d): you copy its path from your own list, sign for sign. If any check fails, do not run the command and tell the assistant. If all hold, run `shred -u -- '<path>'`, with the path in single quotes. If shred is not installed, run the same five checks and then `rm -- '<path>'`.

## When the clean-up may start
G.2. When you are done with parts (a) and (b), write `shred done key copies`; when you are done with parts (c) and (d), write `shred done other files`. `shred done key copies` means that EVERY file of (a) and (b) is shredded, and `shred done other files` that EVERY file of (c) and (d) is shredded, each by this rule (so `shred done key copies` and `shred done other files` together mean every file of (a), (b), (c) and (d)). If even one is not, write `shred not done key copies` or `shred not done other files`, the last part of the name of each file that is left (never its content) and the reason. If you write neither, the assistant treats it as `shred not done key copies` or `shred not done other files`. The assistant never opens the files. Shredding does not revoke the key, and it is not reliable on every disk, so it does not replace key step I. Item 1 starts only when ALL of these are true, each as a message that you typed yourself: a confirmation (`yes, no more takes` or `key gone, no more decisions`; key step D.3); `key revoked` (or `key gone, no more decisions`, which counts as `key revoked`; or `key not revoked` and the reason, but item 1 never starts while a copy of the key exists and the key is not revoked); `shred done key copies` (or `shred not done key copies` and the reason); your items 2, 3, 4 and 5 done; `shred done other files` (or `shred not done other files` and the reason); you have read the list of what the assistant will remove; and `start clean-up`. If by 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST (the assistant has no timer: it reads the time shown on your last message in the chat, and a message with no time shown counts as late) you have not written ALL of these, the assistant does not start item 1 in that session; it lists in the handoff every item that is left, with you as its owner and the date Tue 24 Nov 2026 18:29 UTC = 23:59 IST (this date is to be confirmed by the studio lead before you rely on it) (or a later date that you give) as the date to finish, and asks you at your next session. Item 1 may start in a later session, but still only when ALL of these are true. An item 2 or item 3 that you left under the identification rule counts as done in this list once you wrote `web revokes not done`, its name and the reason. The assistant never does an owner item for you (the web revokes, the shreds, the retire check). Detail: appendix K-G.
