## PENDING LEAD (continues the ticket's numbering 1 to 10)
11. **Overshoot gives a signal, not a global stop** (replaces PL4). Recommended: cut the stop. The meter returns `Settled.overshoot` and calls `on_overshoot`; P4c-2 sets `NEEDS_PERSON run_budget` for that run and writes the audit row. Reason: P4d now books the full cost, so nothing is under-counted. One wrong estimate, possibly caused by an attacker's input, would otherwise stop every agent.
12. **The budget day is fixed at UTC.** Cut `cap_zone` and `Config.meter`. Recommended: yes. A configurable zone becomes v1.1, changed in four places together, with the trigger "a client asks for a non-UTC budget day".
13. **P4b1-c leaves this ticket** and becomes a separate ticket after PL7(a)-(d) are answered. Recommended: yes. Until then the demo keeps its file meter. That is safe offline; for its live route, the existing PL52 rule "no live demo call until P4b1-c" holds.
14. **Run scope for the wrapper:** a context variable opened by `metered.scope(...)`; with no scope, the call is refused. Recommended: yes. P4c-2 rev 2 aligns with it. The alternative, one wrapper per run, cannot reach the judge's shared `deps.llm`.
15. **Exit-settle failure** (amends PL3). Recommended: retry `StoreBusy` twice, then keep the reservation; on `counter_damaged` the caller stops that agent and a person repairs it and resends the logged settle; the paid reply is still returned. This follows P4d PL23.
16. **Prompt bound of 100,000 tokens**, refused before the reserve. Recommended: yes, as a named constant. The number comes from the repo cost-rules file and is UNVERIFIED against an official price page; the lead checks it before any live call.
17. **Failed-call booking (PL60).** Recommended: book `request count x attempts x W` with P4a's fallback attempts constant, with no `on_overshoot`. Consequence in plain words: a failing call with 3 attempts books 3 times its reservation and can take a counter past its cap by up to 2W, after which the next reserve is refused. P4b2-a later lowers this per attempt.
