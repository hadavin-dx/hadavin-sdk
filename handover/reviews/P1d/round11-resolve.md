### G3 (readiness M2) Put-back count: one formula: PROPOSE FIX
- **Finding:** "count of each key of k in each key of p" leaves sum, max or min open; M38 flips "smallest" to "largest".
- **Decision:** take P1b's own rule from `lookalike-leak.md` section 5 item 3, unchanged. For each known every-copy value k with at least one key of `scan_keyset(k)` of length `TN_MIN` or more (its **needle keys**), each needle key n and each key s of `scan_keyset(p)` (p = the put-back form of shown): `c(s, n) = s.count(n)` (Python `str.count`, non-overlapping); `e(n) = sum over the distinct put-back originals O of m(O) x min over t in scan_keyset(O) of t.count(n)`, where m(O) = how many of O's placeholders were put back and the minimum over an empty key set is 0; **residue when `c(s, n) > e(n)` for any k, n, s.** The SMALLEST count, because `scan_keyset` is a `frozenset` with no label of which reading made which key, so a same-reading count cannot be computed and the smallest never over-explains (section 5 item 3, rule (ii)). M38 (largest) and M63 (`>=`) keep their meaning; their killers are chosen at BS-0 (q) as before.
