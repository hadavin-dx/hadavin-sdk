## Positions
1. **M1 is fixed in the trigger, not in the function (option b).** Every OFF to ON update goes through the trigger: the reset's pre-step, the DBA recipe, an old-release upsert after a rollback. A label left from the last reset is never "who stopped". The probe shows the fix and shows that an explicit name survives. The request table stays the one record of who stopped (`cleared_by`).
2. **Set `lock_timeout` after the migration lock, never before.** Before it, two honest `migrate` runs fail each other (P8-4). After it, the bound on how long a stop can wait behind DDL is unchanged.
3. **A reset can finish while a stop is in flight, and the switch is then ON again.** That is correct, fail-safe behaviour. The CLI says so by re-reading once; the audit keeps `reset`.
