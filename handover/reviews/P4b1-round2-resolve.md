## Positions (plain words)
- **Two Majors are contested with evidence a reader can open.**
  - ADV-3: no core kind can send an image or a PDF; the code decodes every document to text.
  - ADV-4: P4a refuses cache tokens before a reply exists.
  - For both, the cheap and lasting answer is a pin row (B20) and one ticket sentence, not new refusal code. If a kind ever sends image blocks, B20 fails first.
- **Surface cut, not added, where possible.**
  - The release rule becomes a table with fewer special cases ("release when the outcome is unknown").
  - The unknown-attempt count moves to P4b2's `Reply` field. P4b1 needs no attribute on the inner model.
  - The new names are two constants, one floor constant and two log lines. No new method, field or class.
- **Money direction.** Every decision books equal or higher, never lower.
  - The 1 micro-dollar floor over-books at most 1 micro-dollar per request.
  - The `StoreBusy` release closes a hold that was never sent.
  - The failure-booking bound refuses before any spend.
- **What I did not take.** ADV-3's `ValueError` for non-text documents (nothing can produce one) and ADV-4's "refuse cache markers" (P4a already does it).
