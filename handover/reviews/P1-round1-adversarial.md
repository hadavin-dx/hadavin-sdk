## Considered and found sound (reviewer's list)
- Pure functions, no I/O, stdlib only (`unicodedata`, `re`, `enum`, `hashlib`, `html`), and no new dependency. The ICU outline with a trigger meets the single-dependency rule.
- `AllowVerdict.__bool__` raising `TypeError` and `allow_equal` as exact `a == b` is the correct ALLOW design (no normalisation on the allow path).
- `FOLD_VERSION` including `unidata_version`, the table digest, the algorithm id and the UTS #39 version is the right idea, apart from A4, A9 and A17. Refusing a version mismatch loudly (never `False`) is the right direction.
- The tag-decode, dash/slash and gap-2 probes are well evidenced. The 56 vs 49 difference is honestly reported as UNVERIFIED.
- Module-level tables are built at import, so no thread sees a half-filled digit table (PR50 `textnorm.py` line 60).
- `P1b depends on P1a`, and the hot-file claim (only `CHANGELOG.md` and `defaults.py`) is plausible.
