## Positions
1. Design (b) stands: refuse the whole text when a key of a known value occurs in the folded text after masking. Draft 4 masks MORE before the check (case-insensitive sweep) and reads MORE in the check (decoded form on every path), with FEWER parts (two key types instead of three; at most two checked texts per call instead of four).
2. **The owner's table, row by row, for the three ways V is known (MAP, PW, MANY), after draft 4:** Cyrillic/Greek, combining mark, split lines: refused on every path; full-width, zero-width: masked on `redact_text` (canonical view), refused on bare paths; other case: MASKED on every path (sweep); every byte percent-encoded, Cyrillic spelling: refused on every path (decoded form); plain letters percent-encoded: masked on `redact_text` (view adopted), refused on bare paths. For every kind, including NAME/ADDRESS/TENANT, within the minimums (secrets: key 8; others: raw 12 and key 8). `redact_many` is read as one joined text.
3. **What stays open (stated, each with a row and a v1.1 trigger):** values under the minimums; markup and identifier-kind fillers; Ze with fillers in a secret; digit-shaped and Latin-only swaps (leetspeak); the mixed Eta reading; encodings other than the layers `undo` decodes (base64, hex, rot13, reversed); copies split across separate strings of different calls; JSON numbers; values not yet known; Warden.
4. No item is left open for the lead to close the order-11 table; the lead CONFIRMS the cost choices (PL-LK-16, -17, -19, -22).
