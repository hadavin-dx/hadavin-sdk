**Verified (read in code).**
- `Runtime.handle` makes a fresh `Run` every call and has no `run_id` parameter (`agent_loop.py:325-348`). `create_run` raises `StaleState` on a repeated id in both stores (`memory.py:51-54`, `postgres.py:238-242`).
- `hmac_v0.verify` signs only `ts` and `body`, caps the age at 300 s either way, and refuses an empty secret (`hmac_v0.py:26-51`). So replay within 300 s verifies again, as the ticket says.
- `Limits.blocked` returns kill_switch, daily_budget and monthly_budget (R1-ADV-1).
- `tick.STEPS` is appendable. `_each` skips `StaleState`. `stuck` moves RECEIVED/WORKING runs to NEEDS_PERSON (`tick.py:112-132`, `:249-266`).
- `GRANTS` uses `_RW` and `_APPEND`, with `purge` and `purge_cols` as a mechanism (`migrate.py:24-68`). `dead_letters` has no foreign key to runs, so a dead letter for a run that never started is possible.
- `tests/contracts/test_store.py` exists.
- The row and mutant counts in the ticket are internally consistent (44 and 34).
- All 17 template fields are present and in order. The weak ones are field 14 (the evidence commands refer to the "P1m runner command line", UNVERIFIED) and field 19 (see R1-ADV-20).
- The outbound rule is met as written: every webhook reply has an empty body, and the schedule sends nothing, so no path needs the AK1 or AK-G22 gate (order 27 item 63). The 409 and 503 responses reveal only status codes. No secret-like literals in the ticket.
