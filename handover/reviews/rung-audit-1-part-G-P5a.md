## Ticket size against the code-last rule

`tickets/P5a.md` is 340 lines and 114,808 bytes before BN-23 (the lead's figure was 330; the extra lines are BN-20 to BN-22). The body before the build notes is 242 lines, just under the "about 250 lines" mark of order 56 item 3(d), but only because lines are very long (average about 340 characters, many over 1,000). By bytes it is about 70 percent of AK-G16 (164 KB), the size the lead called a symptom. Where the size comes from: 2 source files, 9 test and data files, 163 corpus rows (134 active, 27 later, 2 not applicable), 21 codes, 20 fact keys, a mutant appendix. The code is small, the test material is large.

Candidate to split? Yes in shape, no in value. A natural cut is source plus its 3 unit test files (codes, diagnose, render, validation_text) versus corpus, oracle, old-writers and mutants (test-only). I do not recommend it: revision 3 is frozen after three rounds, a split reopens the freeze, the hostile proof needs the corpus to call the code finished, and R450(n) names splitting to stay under a threshold as a Major finding, so a split for that reason would be wrong. The honest trims, both below the 80-percent line and so only proposals, are the old-writers file moving to P5b3 and the 27 `later` rows being added by the packages that own them. PROPOSED, NOT APPLIED: waits for the lead/owner.
