## Considered and found sound (reviewer's list)
- Switch-first, then audit, on stop. Audit-first on reset, with the switch left ON if the audit fails.
- The Postgres missing row reads ON, and the gate treats any store exception as ON.
- `cache_ttl` default 0, and only the stale-allow direction is affected (monotonic clock used).
- A `StopGate(None)` refusal, the class name only in `error_code`, and the flag set before the durable write in `engage`.
- Putting the new errors in `limits.py` and keeping off `errors.py`; adding no migration.
- The weak-row diagnosis and its two-part fix, and the per-sub-PR evidence approach.
- Deferring agent-killswitch scopes and levels, and not copying its 3,756 lines.
