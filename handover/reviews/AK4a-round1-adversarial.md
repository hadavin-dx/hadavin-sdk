Spend checks against the fake ledger versus the core counter (the question you asked): the approach is sound for catching an under-counting meter, and it is better than comparing with the core counter. Four conditions hold:
- The `timeout` price (`billed_timeout`) is chosen by the test, so for timeouts it is an assumption (R1-ADV-7).
- The ledger only counts attempts that reach the fake, so it cannot see a counter that over-counts or a call the core never made.
- AK4a-2's single-process C-SPEND does not touch a core counter (R1-ADV-4).
- The many-process variant depends on P4b1 and P4d-b, which are UNVERIFIED.
