## Positions
1. **Fix the join with one character, not with a P1 export (M-A).** The space restores the property the count rule rests on (key of the whole equals the keys of the pieces). The reviewer's predicate option adds P1 surface and a self-refusal class. The "compute on the real form" option cannot work, because the model sees a token, not letters.
2. **Measure the simpler rule before cutting to it (M-D).** The window rule with tokens dropped is the smaller design and closes more hard rows (M-A, LK-INSERT, LK-PART, self-refusal), but it trades one known leak class for another and has an unmeasured, plausibly high, word-like false-refusal cost. Order 8 says cut surface. Order 11 says close the table. The decision rule is written now, so P-1's numbers decide and not a default.
3. **Benign means benign (M-C).** Refused variant copies are catches, and the Greek and Cyrillic set is built to self-refuse. Neither may push NAME/ADDRESS out for every client. A few hundred texts cannot prove a rate of 1 in 200, so the bound is printed.
4. **Say exactly what is not caught, and only that (M-B, M-3, N3).** The owner text lists the whole copies that are still sent. It stops saying "caught by nothing" where a scrubber does catch something, and stops saying "added" where an end-added character is caught.
