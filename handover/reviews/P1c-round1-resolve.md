## Positions
1. **Emit only what this call placed (COD-1).** Decoded bytes are untrusted whatever the caller is, so the step neutralises them itself. A placeholder is taken into the output only because the redactor just placed it.
2. **Read bytes the way standard tools do, not by a ratio (DES-4).** A printable-percentage gate is a dial an attacker turns with junk bytes. The `strings` rule (every printable piece of 6 or more goes to the redactor) has no such dial. Over-masking was measured at 0 on random binary and identifiers.
3. **Delete a bound that the text cap already gives (DES-3, DES-4 L3; order 8).** A maximum token length and a candidate count bound nothing that `MAX_REDACT_CHARS` and `REDACT_BUDGET_S` do not, and each was a bypass or a refusal of harmless text. The order's bound of depth 1 is kept, because it is the order's decision and it does bound work.
4. **Alignment, not cut lists (DES-1).** Four decodes per run handle every glued prefix exactly and linearly. A list of cut points is a bound with its own bypass.
5. **A reviewer's leak claim is checked against today's output (DES-2).** It is contested only with a probe and the anchor, and the outcome is pinned by a row.
