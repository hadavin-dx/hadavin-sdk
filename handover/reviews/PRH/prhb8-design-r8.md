It stays unique only when: text has a sequence, the character reading was skipped because `leaves_text(text)` is true, an ignorable or bell character exists, and the reading differs from both consuming and keeping readings (a lone ESC plus a letter-final nF sequence in one text, e.g. ESC K together with ESC ( B; the grammar's `strip` eats the letter after ESC K and keeps the B after ESC ( B). A one-cut-at-a-time generator may not build that. `others = (..., fj)` at `:1135` is separate and live. Fix: construct the input and pin it with a mutant, or delete the append, its mutants and the docstring mention with the derivation written down; never delete on the sample. `find` folds six readings (grammar, consuming, keeping, character, ignorable-less, mixed), which is why withheld rose from 143 to 155 of 3,000.
