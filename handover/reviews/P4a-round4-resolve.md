## Positions
1. **Blocker design.** Strict helper for the reservation, TOTAL helper for the charge. Rejected: making `request_bound` itself total (it would hide a missing declaration from the reservation, the one place a loud refusal is wanted); rejected: leaving the crash as "intended" (it contradicts the function's own docstring after a paid call).
2. **Wrappers.** Forward the two attributes; the total helper makes a forgotten forward visible (a log line) and over-charging, never a crash and never a silent single-attempt charge.
3. **Billed-unknown charges.** One rule for every branch that follows an attempt that may have been billed: `requests x attempts`, with the fallback constants. The narrower exhausted rule is offered to the lead (PL31), not adopted.
4. **Zero output and zero price.** Both refused where the evidence says a zero cannot be true (content with 0 output; a live route priced 0).
5. **Tiers.** Control is a number in P4b1's pre-call bound, gated before merge. No tier code.
6. **Order-7 check:** every addition above removes or closes a concrete way to under-charge or to crash after a paid call; no new public interface except two private constants and one private helper.
