What (a) gives up, said plainly: old-key receipts keep only the database's INSERT protections, not the body-hash re-check. Also stated: with `hkey = None` (demo, no restored key) the body check is plain SHA-256, which anyone with database credentials can recompute. Rows: L20 gains (vii); L24 gains two cases.
