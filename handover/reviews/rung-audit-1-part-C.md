**Cross-ticket note (AK2b, AK2c, AK4b, not audited here).** A managed knowledge-base service in the client's cloud (rung 2) can do chunking, embedding and retrieval. It cannot be the default if it receives raw document bytes: AK0's J2 rule says such content "bypasses mask" (`AK0.md:29`). The one hybrid that keeps our guarantee: our code reads, quality-checks and MASKS, then uploads only masked text; the service chunks, embeds and retrieves. Whether its metadata filters enforce per-reader access is AK2c's isolation question. This hybrid could cut part of AK2b/AK2c. It does NOT cut AK2a, because reading, the quality report and masking stay ours.
