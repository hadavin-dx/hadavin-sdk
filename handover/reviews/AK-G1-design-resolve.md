## Proposed owner-visible-limits sentences (plain words; the lead edits `owner-visible-limits.md`)

1.  "If one answer takes longer than the agent's time limit (about 16 minutes with the normal model settings), the system treats that answer as lost. A person takes over that chat, and the chat history shows '[no answer was given]'. The customer may already have received that slow answer, or may still receive it shortly after, because a reply that is already being sent is not pulled back."
2.  "If the database or the audit store fails for a moment, every chat that was being answered at that moment goes to a person, not just one."
3.  "When a chat pauses because of a money limit, one alert card is posted. If the system crashes in the moment after the chat pauses, that card can be missing; the run record still shows the money reason."
4.  "Deleting a person's chats is refused while one of their messages is being answered. After a crash this can last up to about 16 minutes, and the caller must try again." (amends the ticket's limit 2(vi))
5.  "After a change to which kinds of personal data are kept, or to the deployment's secret key, a message sent again under its old message id may be refused. The sending app must send it again under a new id."
6.  Only if the lead keeps NEEDS LEAD 2 (a): "A chat that waits longer than the retention period (30 days unless the client contract says otherwise) for a person is deleted with its history, even if no person has answered it yet."
7.  For operators, not for the owner's list: "All machines of one deployment must keep their clocks within 60 seconds of each other."
