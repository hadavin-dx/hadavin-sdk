## 1. The question, in plain words
A value V that the redactor already knows is replaced by a placeholder when written plainly. The same V written with look-alike letters, other case, one combining mark, zero-width characters inside, full-width forms, or split over two lines goes to the model unchanged. What is the smallest design that makes such a copy impossible to send?
**Two answers, in this order.** (1) A small set of copies is MASKED in place like plain copies: other-case and re-spaced copies of stored NAME and ADDRESS values (the case pass, section 5 step 0). (2) Any other copy that a fold of the text still reads as V makes the redactor REFUSE the whole text (the check, section 5).
**What "a folded copy" means in this note (exactly):** a text one of whose scan keys contains a scan key of V. The scan key is `scan_keyset` of P1b (NFKD and the confusables table, case folded in two readings, ALL whitespace removed, class map `z` to `3`, tag texts T0-T3). The text is read as written AND after `runmap.undo` (JSON, backslash, percent and entity layers decoded). Anything a reader can see as V only through a transformation outside these two functions is NOT covered (section 10).
**Scope, stated plainly.** (a) This closes the table for the core repository (`hadavin_sdk`). Warden's own `redaction.redact` is in another repository, which I did not read; it is open until the lead decides PL-LK-24. (b) Stored NAME and ADDRESS values are NOT in the check in v1 (PL-LK-26): their other-case and re-spaced copies are masked, but a copy of a stored name or address written with look-alike letters, a combining mark or accents goes to the model. Reason: in ordinary letters and invoices the same name or address appears with different line breaks, a longer surname (`Anna Schmidtke` for a stored `Anna Schmidt`) or a longer house number, and the check would end those runs; the cost is named in section 7 and the lead confirms it. Every other known value (secrets and identifiers, with a raw length of 12 or more) is covered on every path in the table.

## 3. Three designs compared
**(a) Span-mask.** Fold, find V's key, map the hit back to written positions, replace only that span. Needs a position map; `_aligned_fold` (redact.py 1082) gives one only for simple folds and returns None otherwise (COR-R4-4), and then the line is withheld anyway. P2d-1's alias index is a smaller version of this idea and drew findings in rounds 3 to 5 (ADV-R3-2, ADV-R3-3, ADV-R3-8, ADV-R5-5, ADV-R5-6). Best fidelity, largest surface. Rejected for v1 (v1.1 LKV-1). The case pass of section 5 is NOT this: it is a literal regular-expression search on the written text (`re.IGNORECASE`), so its match positions are written positions; no folded hit is mapped back.
**(b) Refuse the whole text.** After all masking, if a scan key of V occurs in a scan key of the text, refuse. No position map. A person looks.
**(c) The round-4 fallback (`deny_hit` over the final output).** Gone: `deny_hit`, `prepare_needles` and `id_keyset` are cut from P1 v1 (P1 rev 10, PENDING LEAD 27). Its useful part, the scan key, is `scan_keyset`, which (b) calls directly. GOLD-1 ties the check to a plain-`in` oracle over the same keys (section 6).
**Pick: (b), with a case pass for NAME and ADDRESS.** Why: every table row is closed by an `in`-style check on folded keys; the failure is a refusal, not a leak (fail closed); the span machinery that kept failing review is not needed. What it costs: the whole text is lost when it holds a variant of a known value, including an upper-case copy of a known secret. **On the agent-loop paths a refusal ends the run**: the document path reports `redaction_error` (agent_loop 376-377; only `InputTooLarge` is reported as `document_too_large`, 371-375), tool results 469-470, tool inputs 495-496, the proposal check 649-650, all through `_redaction_failed` (547-549: a `guard/redaction failed` note and an abstention). **On the degrade paths it becomes a fixed note**: `audit/capped.py` 72-73 (withheld marker), `tools.py` 1006-1009 and `safe_head` (`SAFE_HEAD_NOTE`), and, new in draft 5, the approval-card path (section 5).
Size, honestly (an ESTIMATE): about 15 lines refactored in `_build` (one shared grouping helper), about 20 new lines for the case pass in `sweep` (existing sweep code unchanged), about 60 new lines in `redact.py` and `runmap.py` (one key type, the check with its decoded form, the joined `redact_many` check, the extra originals, the exception), one keyword parameter through `redact_text`, `redact_untrusted`, `redact_layered` and `prepare_output`, about 4 lines in `error_reason`, about 8 lines in the approval-card path, 3 constants, and the rows of section 6. Draft 4's case-insensitive rewrite of the whole sweep, its key for fillers, the secret-8 tier, the `_clean` change and the second pass of `redact_value` are CUT (round-4 resolve).

## 4. Why a plain fold is not enough (decides the dependency)
My draft-1 prototype used the snapshot `fold` with case folding and all whitespace removed. All table rows were caught, but the table reads some look-alikes as a different character: Cyrillic Ze (U+0437) reads `3`, so a V holding `z` written with Ze is missed; Cyrillic Be (U+0431) reads `6`; small Greek eta (U+03B7) reads `n`. Confirmed in `_confusables.py`. P1b's class map (`z` to `3`) and two case readings close Ze and capital Eta. **So the check needs `scan_keyset`, and P2d-2 FOLLOWS P1b** (order 11; P1 rev 10 "What downstream packages wait for"). P1 keeps no class map for digit-shaped letters (PENDING LEAD 29): a known value that holds the DIGIT and is written with such a source is found; one that holds the LETTER and is written with a digit-shaped source (Be for `b`) is a leetspeak rewrite, not a look-alike, and stays open (LKV-3, xfail LK-BE). Class maps live in P1b only; this design adds none.
P1 rev 10 supplies no percent or JSON decoder (its entity step decodes HTML entities only). The decoded form therefore comes from this design, through the existing `runmap.undo` (runmap.py 139-170), not from P1.
The design uses no other P1 function: `squeeze` and `fold` are not called (the squeeze key is cut in draft 5); `fold` and `squeeze` change in P1b anyway (P1 rev 10, "Kept from PR #50 head", COR-R8-10), which does not affect this design.

## 5. The design (b), exactly
**Step 0, the case pass for NAME and ADDRESS (masking, before any check).** The existing case-sensitive sweep runs first, unchanged, so every exact copy of every stored value keeps its OWN placeholder (two stored values that differ only in case are never merged). Then, for each segment, ONE more search runs over the originals whose kind is in `defaults.LOOKALIKE_WORD_KINDS = ("NAME", "ADDRESS")` and that pass today's `_sweep` rule for non-secret kinds (3+ characters, not all-digit under 6, not a `_COMMON` word): a regular expression made of each such original, escaped, with every run of whitespace inside it replaced by `\s+`, longer first, compiled with `re.IGNORECASE` by `_compile_uncached` (nothing in `re`'s process-wide cache) through the shared grouping helper (the `RecursionError`/`re.error`/`OverflowError` fallback to one-by-one). It searches the WRITTEN segment, so a match's positions are written positions (no lowered copy, no alignment rule). A match is replaced by its original's placeholder with today's standalone rule (`_replace_standalone`'s boundary test); a match that overlaps a placeholder token is skipped. When several stored originals are equal ignoring case and spacing, a copy that matches none of them exactly gets the first one's placeholder (longer first, then insertion order), and `restore` gives that spelling (fidelity, not a leak; row LK-CASE2). `re.IGNORECASE` folds per character: both Greek sigma forms match each other (probed), `İ` does not shift positions (probed), and a case change that changes the length (`STRASSE` for `Straße`) is not matched (probed; section 10). Effect: all-capitals, lower-case and Title Case copies of stored names and addresses, and copies re-broken over lines or with doubled spaces, are MASKED on every path. **Not in the case pass, on purpose:** secret kinds (an other-case copy of a secret is nearly always deliberate; the check refuses it, ADV-LK4-15) and every other identifier kind (an object name or path on a case-sensitive system names a DIFFERENT object in other case; masking it would make `restore` and the grounding check of `runtime/evidence.py` 85-110 read the stored spelling, ADV-LK4-1; e-mail, UUID and host patterns are case-insensitive already, redact.py 243-246, 263, 395, so their other-case copies are masked by the pattern itself).
**New public function** `check_lookalike(texts, mapping, *, extra=(), _cache=None, _deadline=None) -> None` in `guard/redact.py`; it raises **`LookalikeCopy`**, a new public subclass of `runmap.RedactionError` defined in `runmap.py` and raised from `redact.py` through a function-level import (the precedent: `_Redactor._over_budget`, redact.py 986-992, raises `InputTooLarge` the same way; `runmap.undo` is imported the same way). Message = class name only. Bare callers that catch `RedactionError` catch it. Its only exception handler names `TextnormError` (a hit) and is in `check_lookalike` itself, the name listed in P1's `REFUSAL_HANDLERS` (P1 rev 10, "the caller rule and the refusal lint" (b)); there is no `except Exception` around a textnorm call (lint (a); COR-R8-9: the finder's own fallback handler wraps no textnorm call). Internally a private method `_Redactor.lookalike_hit(text)` shares the built state.
1. **Values and keys.** Every original of `mapping` except `"\0seed"` entries and except originals whose kind is in `LOOKALIKE_WORD_KINDS`, plus every string in `extra` (treated as a secret; never added to the map). There is no list of included kinds: a kind added to the redactor later is checked by default (ADV-LK4-9). **One length rule for every kind:** the RAW original has at least `defaults.LOOKALIKE_MIN_RAW_CHARS = 12` characters (`len(original)`, spaces counted) AND each key of `scan_keyset(original)` has at least `defaults.LOOKALIKE_MIN_KEY_CHARS = 8` characters; a key under 8 is dropped. So a secret of 12 or more characters, a 36-character UUID, a 14-character id written with spaces are covered; an 11-character SSN-shaped value, a 10-character PAN and any secret of 8 to 11 characters are not (xfail LK-SHORT; LKV-2). Keys are built once per original and kept in a per-run memo `cache["lookalike_keys"]` (original -> its keys, or the marker "unfoldable"; in the run's cache, never module-level, R371). **An original that `scan_keyset` refuses (`TextnormError`) counts as a hit: the check raises `LookalikeCopy` for every checked text while that value is in the map** (fail closed; P1 rev 10 error contract, ADV-R8-5, PENDING LEAD 34; row LK-UNFOLD). Cost: one unfoldable stored value ends the run at the next check (stated, section 10).
2. **Finder.** The grouping code of `_build` (nesting measure, `_split`, `_slow`, the fallback to one-by-one) is factored into ONE helper used by the sweep finder, the case-pass finder and the look-alike finder. The look-alike state is cached as `cache["lookalike"] = (map snapshot, extra, finder, slow)` and reused only when the snapshot EQUALS the current map and `extra` is equal (never keyed by length: a clear-and-update to a different same-size map would reuse a stale finder, a leak). Finders are rebuilt when the map changes, the same rate as the sweep's own rebuild (redact.py 1310); thanks to the memo only new originals are folded. With a plain dict and no cache (bare callers), keys are rebuilt on every call (cost printed by FR-15; nothing survives the call, row LK-NOCACHE).
3. **Text, and its decoded form.** For each checked text t, the check reads t and, when different, `runmap.undo(t)` (JSON `\uXXXX` and one-character escapes, backslash escapes, percent-encoding, HTML entities, NFKC and invisible characters, to a fixed point; nested deeper than `defaults.SCRUB_MAX_LAYERS` it raises `TooDeep`, a `RedactionError`, which propagates: fail closed). In each, every genuine placeholder (a token matching `redact._PLACEHOLDER` AND a key of the map) is replaced by ONE ASCII space; any other `<UPPER_n>` token is ordinary text (today `sweep` skips every such token, so `<ABCD1234EFGH_5>`, an upper-case spelling of an alphanumeric value, survives; this closes it, row LK-FAKEPH). The result goes through `scan_keyset` once; every key is searched with the finder. `scan_keyset` removes whitespace, so **the pieces either side of a placeholder are joined on purpose**: a copy of V that the attacker splits by writing a maskable value (an e-mail, an IP) in its middle is read (row LK-PH). A space, not deletion, so that the pieces cannot form a new entity or combining sequence across the junction before whitespace is removed (row LK-PH-ENT). (No stand-in character: P1b drops U+FFFD, SK-6 and RES-1.) A hit returns the placeholder of the matching value, or the fixed label `<HANDED_SECRET>` for an `extra` value (for the log line; never the value). An empty key set returns at once (no `undo`, no key call).
4. **Fail closed and budget.** `TextTooLong`, `NotText` and any other `TextnormError`, from the text or from an original, count as a hit (`LookalikeCopy`). Every other exception propagates: `redact_text` turns a non-`RedactionError` into `RedactionError` (its existing handler around the `redact()` calls); a bare caller gets the exception and no text. `scan_keyset` has no clock (P1 rev 10 SK-4): the budget `deadline` (the caller's `_deadline`, or for a bare call one the check starts itself, `time.thread_time() + REDACT_BUDGET_S`) is checked before `undo` and before every `scan_keyset` call, and past it the check raises `InputTooLarge`. `REDACT_BUDGET_S = 6.0` is CPU seconds of the calling thread (`time.thread_time()`, runmap.py 484); the `defaults.py` comment that says "wall-clock" is wrong and P2d-2 corrects it. The longest uninterrupted step is one `scan_keyset` call (S_call, measured by the P1b evidence runner; about 2 s by multiplication, UNVERIFIED), or one `undo` of a text at the cap (UNVERIFIED). Added work per `redact_text`: at most 2 checked texts (`out` and `undo(out)`) x one `scan_keyset` call. The check shares the redactor's budget; whether that fits is measured BEFORE P2d-2 is built (section 7, precondition P-2): if it needs more than half the budget on a benign document at the cap, the check gets its own budget of the same size (one constant, added then).

## 7. False-refusal rate and cost: what is and is not known, and two preconditions
Not measured on real documents; no figure is guessed. Known: (1) after masking, a PLAIN copy of V is normally a placeholder. The sweep leaves plain copies in three production cases (an identifier inside a longer ASCII letter/digit run, words in `_COMMON`, values under 3 characters or all-digit under 6); only the first produces refusals (`_COMMON` words are at most 10 characters and short values are under the minimum). (2) Chance matches are negligible for random secrets and ids: the draft-1 prototype found 0 hits for 8,000 random 12-40-character keys in 262,144 characters; by arithmetic, a random 8-character key matches about 262,144 / 36^8 = 9e-8 per text per value, a 12-digit number about 2.6e-7. (3) Word kinds are out of the check, so the word-key risks of draft 4 (accented names, joined word keys in prose, a longer surname or house number, an address re-broken over lines) cause no refusal (FR-17, FR-24, FR-29; LK-NAME-SPLIT is masked). (4) The named risks that remain: other-case copies of secrets and of case-sensitive identifiers (LK-UP, FR-30), identifiers glued to letters or digits (FR-11, FR-23), long quoted captures (FR-19), split copies around a placeholder (FR-21), Mn-only differences (FR-5), an unfoldable stored value (LK-UNFOLD).
**Precondition P-1 (false refusals; ADV-LK4-11).** After P1b merges and before P2d-2 is built (the lead's `S0 EXIT` for P2d-2 waits for it), an evidence runner runs a throwaway prototype of the check on P1b's `scan_keyset` (not repository code) over a made-up corpus: 200 invoices and letters, 100 tool results (ERP/database style with padded columns, one with ANSI colour), 100 model tool-call arguments; stored names, addresses, tenant ids, cloud resource ids and passwords of 8 to 16 characters, some dictionary words, written plain, in capitals, padded, re-broken and accented. Output: the refusal rate per kind, the masked-by-case-pass count, and the same corpus with NAME and ADDRESS checked (to inform PL-LK-26), in `handover/evidence/lookalike-1.md`. **Gate:** more than 1 refusal in 50 documents for a kind: that kind is added to `LOOKALIKE_WORD_KINDS` (masked case and spacing copies, variants open) and a v1.1 row opens; the lead may instead keep it in the check. If NAME and ADDRESS checked give at most 1 refusal in 50 documents, the lead may take them back into the check (one-line change of the constant).
**Precondition P-2 (time; ADV-LK4-14).** The same runner: 200 benign documents near the size cap, ASCII and non-ASCII, with a 500-entry map; time per part (`undo`, each `scan_keyset` call, the finder, the case pass) in thread CPU seconds. If the check needs more than half of `REDACT_BUDGET_S` on any of them, P2d-2 gives the check its own budget of the same size (one constant) and the CHANGELOG says a call can take up to twice the budget.

|---|---|---|---|---|

PHONE (redact.py 478-485) is `\+?\d(?:[\d \-]){8,14}\d`: 10 to 16 characters, digits with spaces or hyphens inside and a digit at each end; it must not follow a letter, digit, `_`, `.` or `-`, must not be followed by a letter, digit, `_` or `.`, and is not an ISO date or a 14-digit compact timestamp.
