## 1. The question, in plain words
A value V that the redactor already knows is replaced by a placeholder when written plainly. The same V written with look-alike letters, other case, one combining mark, zero-width characters inside, full-width forms, or split over two lines goes to the model unchanged. What is the smallest design that makes such a copy impossible to send?
**Two answers, in this order.** (1) Copies the redactor can find with a plain literal search in any simple letter case are MASKED in place, like plain copies (the sweep, made case-insensitive, section 5). (2) Any other copy that a fold of the text still reads as V makes the redactor REFUSE the whole text (the check, section 5).
**What "a folded copy" means in this note (exactly):** a text one of whose keys contains a key of V, where a key is one of two things, each made by an existing P1 function: the **scan key** (`scan_keyset`, P1b: NFKD and the confusables table, case folded in two readings, ALL whitespace removed, class map `z` to `3`, tag texts T0-T3; used for every kind); the **squeeze key** (`squeeze` of P1a: upper case, every character other than `A-Z0-9` removed; used in addition for secret kinds). The text is read as written AND after `runmap.undo` (JSON, backslash, percent and entity layers decoded). Anything a reader can see as V only through a transformation outside these functions is NOT covered (section 10).

## 3. Three designs compared
**(a) Span-mask.** Fold, find V's key, map the hit back to written positions, replace only that span. Needs a position map; `_aligned_fold` (redact.py 1082) gives one only for simple folds and returns None otherwise (COR-R4-4), and then the line is withheld anyway. P2d-1's alias index is a smaller version of this idea and drew findings in rounds 3 to 5 (ADV-R3-2, ADV-R3-3, ADV-R3-8, ADV-R5-5, ADV-R5-6). Best fidelity, largest surface. Rejected for v1 (v1.1 LKV-1). Note: the case-insensitive sweep of section 5 is NOT this: it finds a KNOWN original's own simple-case spelling at the same index of a same-length lowered copy of the segment; no folded hit is mapped back.
**(b) Refuse the whole text.** After all masking, if a key of V occurs in a key of the text, refuse. No position map. A person looks.
**(c) The round-4 fallback (`deny_hit` over the final output).** Gone: `deny_hit`, `prepare_needles` and `id_keyset` are cut from P1 v1 (P1 rev 9, "Cut to v1.1", PENDING LEAD 27). Its useful part, the scan key, is `scan_keyset`, which (b) calls directly. The GOLD-1 row ties the check to a plain-`in` oracle over the same keys (section 6).
**Pick: (b), with the sweep made case-insensitive first.** Why: every table row is closed by an `in`-style check on folded keys; the failure is a refusal, not a leak (fail closed); the span machinery that kept failing review is not needed; and the most common variant, other case, is masked rather than refused, so it costs no run. What it costs: the whole text is lost when it holds a non-case variant of a known value. **On the agent-loop paths a refusal ends the run**: the document path reports `redaction_error` (agent_loop 376-377; only `InputTooLarge` is reported as `document_too_large`, 371-375), tool results 469-470, tool inputs 495-496, the proposal check 649-650, all through `_redaction_failed` (547-549: a `guard/redaction failed` note and an abstention). **On the degrade paths it becomes a fixed note**: `audit/capped.py` 72-73 (withheld marker), `tools.py` 1006-1009 and `safe_head` (`SAFE_HEAD_NOTE`). That price decides sections 5 and 7.
Size, honestly (an ESTIMATE): a refactor of about 15 lines in `_build` (shared grouping helper), about 25 lines changed in `_build`/`sweep` for the case-insensitive search, about 90 new lines in `redact.py` and `runmap.py` (two key types, the check with its decoded form, the joined `redact_many` check, the second pass of `redact_value`, the exception, the `_clean` change), about 6 lines restructured in `redact_text` (map update before raising), 4 constants, and the rows of section 6.

## 4. Why a plain fold is not enough (decides the dependency)
My draft-1 prototype used the snapshot `fold` with case folding and all whitespace removed. All table rows were caught, but the table reads some look-alikes as a different character: Cyrillic Ze (U+0437) reads `3`, so a V holding `z` written with Ze is missed; Cyrillic Be (U+0431) reads `6`; small Greek eta (U+03B7) reads `n`. Confirmed in `_confusables.py`. P1b's class map (`z` to `3`) and two case readings close Ze and capital Eta. **So the check needs `scan_keyset`, and P2d-2 FOLLOWS P1b** (order 11; P1 rev 9 "Downstream split"). P1 rev 9 keeps no class map for digit-shaped letters (PENDING LEAD 29): a known value that holds the DIGIT and is written with such a source is found; one that holds the LETTER and is written with a digit-shaped source (Be for `b`) is a leetspeak rewrite, not a look-alike, and stays open (LKV-3, xfail LK-BE). Class maps live in P1b only; this design adds none (section 10, mixed Eta reading).
P1 rev 9 ("What P1 supplies to the look-alike check") supplies no percent or JSON decoder (its entity step decodes HTML entities only). The decoded form therefore comes from this design, through the existing `runmap.undo` (runmap.py 139-170), not from P1.

## 5. The design (b), exactly
**Step 0, the sweep becomes case-insensitive (masking, before any check).** `_build` keys its finders and `_rules` by `low1(original)` and `sweep` searches `low1(segment)`, where `low1(s)` is `s.lower()` when that keeps the length of `s`, else a per-character lower that keeps any character whose lower case is longer than one character (such as `İ`) as it is. So index i of `low1(segment)` is index i of `segment` by construction: a hit is replaced in the WRITTEN segment at the same index, with today's rule (`_sweep`: every copy of a secret of 4+ characters; every standalone copy of other kinds of 3+ characters, not all-digit under 6, not a `_COMMON` word). `_replace_standalone` takes the lowered segment for the search and the written segment for the boundary test and the output. When a placeholder is inserted, the same placeholder text is inserted at the same index of the lowered copy, so both stay aligned. Two stored originals equal under `low1` share one finder entry; the longer-first order decides which placeholder is used, and `restore` gives that spelling (fidelity, not a leak; FR-28). A case change that changes the length (`ß` and `SS`) is not found by the sweep and goes to the check (prototype: `evidence/lookalike-round3-probes.md`, probe2 (a)). Effect: upper, lower and Title Case copies of every kind are masked on every path, including NAME/ADDRESS/TENANT and a planted `password=<word>` whose word later appears in another case.
**New public function** `check_lookalike(texts, mapping, *, _cache=None, _deadline=None) -> None` in `guard/redact.py`; it raises **`LookalikeCopy`**, a new public subclass of `runmap.RedactionError` defined in `runmap.py` and raised from `redact.py` through a function-level import (the precedent: `_Redactor._over_budget`, redact.py 986-992, raises `InputTooLarge` the same way, so there is no import cycle; `runmap.undo` is imported the same way). Message = class name only. Bare callers that catch `RedactionError` catch it. Its only exception handler is for `TextnormError` (a hit), in `check_lookalike` itself, the name listed in P1's `REFUSAL_HANDLERS` lint constant (P1 rev 9 "the caller rule and the refusal lint" (b)); there is no `except Exception` (lint (a)). Internally a private method `_Redactor.lookalike_hit(text)` shares the built state.
1. **Values and keys.** Every original of `mapping` except `"\0seed"` entries, whose kind is in `LOOKALIKE_KINDS` and for which `_sweep(placeholder, original)` is not None. Two key sets, each built once per original and kept in a per-run memo `cache["lookalike_keys"]` (original -> its keys; in the run's cache, never module-level, R371):
   - **K_scan** = the keys of `scan_keyset(original)`, for every kind in `LOOKALIKE_KINDS`, including NAME, ADDRESS and TENANT. Minimums: for `redact.SECRET_KINDS` each key must have at least `defaults.LOOKALIKE_MIN_SECRET_KEY_CHARS = 8` characters; for every other kind the RAW original must have at least `defaults.LOOKALIKE_MIN_KEY_CHARS = 12` characters (`len(original)`, spaces counted) AND each key at least `defaults.LOOKALIKE_MIN_KEY_FLOOR = 8`. So `Anna Schmidt` (raw 12, key 11), `Rue de Paris` (12, 10) and `Main Street 5` (13, 11) are protected. A key under its minimum is dropped.
   - **K_sq** = `squeeze(original)` (P1a, `loose=False`) for `SECRET_KINDS` only, minimum 12 characters of the key. It reads punctuation fillers (dots, hyphens, underscores, U+FFFD) between the letters of a secret (round-2 probe "squeeze"). Not for identifier kinds: squeezing would join host names, IPs and addresses into prose.
   There is no case key any more (draft 3 had one for NAME/ADDRESS/TENANT): it existed only to keep case-only copies from being refused, and those copies are now masked by step 0; it also left an all-capitals copy with one look-alike letter unread. An original that a P1 function refuses (`TextnormError`) is skipped and counted in the evidence step (it is unprotected against variants).
2. **Finder.** The grouping code of `_build` (nesting measure, `_split`, `_slow`, the `RecursionError/re.error/OverflowError` fallback to one-by-one) is factored into ONE helper used by the sweep finder and by one look-alike finder per key set. The look-alike state is cached as `cache["lookalike"] = (map snapshot, finders, slow)` and reused only when the snapshot EQUALS the current map (never keyed by length: a clear-and-update to a different same-size map would reuse a stale finder, a leak). Finders are rebuilt when the map changes, the same rate as the sweep's own rebuild (redact.py 1310); thanks to the memo only new originals are folded. Nothing lives in `re`'s process-wide cache (`_compile_uncached`).
3. **Text, and its decoded form.** For each checked text t, the check reads t and, when different, `runmap.undo(t)` (JSON `\uXXXX` and one-character escapes, backslash escapes, percent-encoding, HTML entities, NFKC and invisible characters, to a fixed point; nested deeper than `defaults.SCRUB_MAX_LAYERS` it raises `TooDeep`, a `RedactionError`, which propagates: fail closed). In each, every genuine placeholder (a token matching `redact._PLACEHOLDER` AND a key of the map) is replaced by ONE ASCII space; any other `<UPPER_n>` token is ordinary text (today `sweep` skips every such token, so `<ABCD1234EFGH_5>`, an upper-case spelling of an alphanumeric value, survives; this closes it, row LK-FAKEPH). The result goes through `scan_keyset` once, and through `squeeze` once when K_sq is non-empty; every key is searched with its finder. Both key functions remove whitespace, so **the pieces either side of a placeholder are joined on purpose**: a copy of V that the attacker splits by writing a maskable value (an e-mail, an IP) in its middle is read (row LK-PH). A space, not deletion, so that the pieces cannot form a new entity or combining sequence across the junction before whitespace is removed (row LK-PH-ENT). (No sentinel: P1b drops U+FFFD, P1 rev 9 SK-6 and RES-1.) A hit returns the placeholder of the matching value (for the log line; never the value). Empty K returns at once (no `undo`, no key call).
4. **Fail closed and budget.** `TextTooLong`, `NotText` and any other `TextnormError` count as a hit (`LookalikeCopy`). Every other exception propagates: `redact_text` turns a non-`RedactionError` into `RedactionError` (its existing handler around the `redact()` calls); a bare caller gets the exception and no text. `scan_keyset` has no clock (P1 rev 9 SK-4): the budget `deadline` (the caller's `_deadline`, or for a bare call one the check starts itself, `time.thread_time() + REDACT_BUDGET_S`) is checked before `undo` and before every key-function call, and past it the check raises `InputTooLarge`. `REDACT_BUDGET_S = 6.0` is CPU seconds of the calling thread (`time.thread_time()`, runmap.py 484); the `defaults.py` comment that says "wall-clock" is wrong and P2d-2 corrects it. The longest uninterrupted step is one call (S_call, measured by the P1b evidence runner; about 2 s by multiplication, UNVERIFIED), or one `undo` of a text at the cap (UNVERIFIED). Added work per `redact_text`: at most 2 checked texts (`out` and `undo(out)`) x at most 2 key-function calls. A hostile text can exceed the budget and is then refused (`InputTooLarge`); a document over P1's size rule is refused when K is non-empty (row FR-16).
5. **`_clean`.** A lone surrogate becomes ONE U+FFFD instead of `?` (runmap.py 393-396), so a copy of an identifier with a lone surrogate inside is joined by the scan key (P1b drops U+FFFD, SK-6). Every caller sees U+FFFD where it saw `?` (CHANGELOG line).
**Call sites (the whole change):**
- `runmap.redact_text`: after the view loop and `_clean`, `check_lookalike([out], current, _cache=cache, _deadline=started + REDACT_BUDGET_S)` on the final `out` only, which is what is sent (its decoded form is read inside the check). Draft 3 also checked the redacted text of every view, adopted or not; that is removed: un-adopted views are never sent, and `undo(out)` reads what a decoder of the sent text reads. Stage 1 and stage 2 `redact()` calls pass `_lookalike=False`, so full-width and zero-width copies are still MASKED by the canonical view and not turned into refusals; a plain copy that is percent-encoded is masked by the adopted `undo` view (PIN LK-PCT-PLAIN). The check uses the GROWN map `current`; on a hit the caller's map is updated BEFORE raising, so a value first seen in this text is stored (row LK-LEARN). Today `mapping.clear(); mapping.update(current)` run after the `try` (runmap.py 526-527); they move so that they run before `LookalikeCopy` is raised (about 6 lines restructured). `LookalikeCopy` is re-raised as itself (added to the `except (InputTooLarge, TooDeep)` tuple).
- bare `redact`: when `_sweep_copies` is true, the check runs on its output and raises `LookalikeCopy`. No caller in `src/` passes `_sweep_copies=False` (grep: only `redact.py` 874, 887; the comment at 887 that names `redact_many` is stale, `redact_many` never calls `redact()`); with False the function returns found-only text and runs no check (stated for external callers).
- `redact_many`: after its second pass, ONE check on the outputs joined by one space (the function redacts its items "as ONE text", redact.py 1530; the join reads a copy split across items, round-3 probe P4; every item's keys are substrings of the joined keys, so no per-item check is needed). A refusal does not name the item.
- Behaviour change for bare callers (they got unmasked text; they get `LookalikeCopy`, or `TooDeep`/`InputTooLarge` from the decoded form): CHANGELOG, loud-failure test; `guard/__init__.py:3` re-exports `redact`, so the CHANGELOG names that import path. Consumers: no bare `redact` or `redact_many` caller under `src/` other than `runmap.py` (grep); demos pin a core sha (list UNVERIFIED, not read).
- **`redact_value` second pass:** when the first pass of `_redact_value` grew the map and the value holds more than one string, `_redact_value` runs again over the FIRST pass's output with the grown map; if that second pass grows the map again, it raises `RedactionError` (fail closed). So a value found in a later leaf masks or refuses its copies in earlier leaves (rows LK-LATER, LATER-PLAIN). This also closes a plain-copy leak that exists today. Cost: up to two `redact_text` calls per leaf, each within its own budget; the total stays capped by `MAX_REDACT_CHARS` (`redact_value`, runmap.py 570-578). Idempotence on benign values: one sample passes on `748849c` (round-3 probe P7: identical output, no map growth); in general UNVERIFIED and measured by FR-25.
- Not a call site: `sweep`. It works per segment before the views exist; the check must see the final text.

## 7. False-refusal rate: what is and is not known
Not measured on real documents; no figure is guessed. Known: (1) after masking, a PLAIN copy of V in any simple case is normally a placeholder. The sweep leaves plain copies in three production cases (an identifier inside a longer ASCII letter/digit run, words in `_COMMON`, values under 3 characters or all-digit under 6), and only the first produces refusals: `_COMMON` words are at most 10 characters and the short values are under every minimum. (`_sweep_copies=False` is passed by no caller in `src/`, and then no check runs.) (2) Chance matches are negligible for random secrets and ids: the draft-1 prototype found 0 hits for 8,000 random 12-40-character keys in 262,144 characters; by arithmetic, a random 8-character key matches about 262,144 / 36^8 = 9e-8 per text per value, a 12-digit number about 2.6e-7. NAME, ADDRESS and TENANT keys are words, not random: the arithmetic does not apply to them (FR-24). (3) The real risks are named: accented spellings of stored names (FR-17), joined word keys in prose (FR-24), all-capitals copies with odd whitespace (FR-26), identifiers glued to letters or digits (FR-11, FR-23), long quoted captures (FR-19), split copies around a placeholder (FR-21), Mn-only differences (FR-5), the second pass on benign values (FR-25). Case-only copies of every kind are masked, not refused. Evidence step (runner): 200 made-up invoices and letters, 100 made-up tool results (ERP/database style with padded columns, one with ANSI colour), 100 made-up model tool-call arguments, with stored names and addresses in plain, capitals, padded and accented spelling and stored passwords of 8 to 12 characters, some dictionary words; the refusal rate per kind and per key type, the masked-by-sweep count, and the added time on benign near-cap documents (including `undo` and `low1`), go to `handover/evidence/lookalike-1.md`. **Gate proposal:** more than 1 refusal in 50 documents for a kind removes that kind from `LOOKALIKE_KINDS` (or, for secret kinds, raises their minimum back to 12) and opens a v1.1 row.

## 9. Order 11(5): Intake's plain account number without a context word
Read and probed in the snapshot (round-2 probe D, bare `redact()`, no map, made-up digits):
| digits | standalone | before a full stop | a letter directly before | after a hyphen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8-9 | kept | kept | kept | kept |
| 10, 11, 13-16 | masked (PHONE) | **kept** | kept | kept |
| 12 | masked (ACCOUNTID) | masked | kept | masked |
| 17-18 | kept | kept | kept | kept |
PHONE (redact.py 478-485) is `\+?\d(?:[\d \-]){8,14}\d`: 10 to 16 characters, digits with spaces or hyphens inside and a digit at each end; it must not follow a letter, digit, `_`, `.` or `-`, must not be followed by a letter, digit, `_` or `.`, and is not an ISO date or a 14-digit compact timestamp. (The probe column tests a letter BEFORE the digits only.) ACCOUNTID (360) is `\b\d{12}\b`. BANKACCT (338-356; `_ACCT_WORD` 89 accepts "account no", "acct", "A/C"; 6 to 17 digits, `_ACCT_DIGITS` 93-96) is masked only with bank context: a phrase of `_BANK_CONTEXT` (100-110, e.g. "bank account", a bank code) within `_BANK_WINDOW` = 100 characters (111, `_bank_context` 114-120), or the match starting with "bank", or in `redact_many` and the fold scan a bank word anywhere in the batch/text (`bank_near`, 1056-1059, 1535). "Account No:" and "A/C" are keywords of the pattern but NOT context. HIGHENTROPY needs 40 or more characters with upper case, lower case and a digit (643-650), so a digit string is never caught. IBAN (253-260) is a registry country code, 2 digits and 11-30 letters or digits, with no check-digit validation and no context word. `_redact_value` returns int and float unchanged (runmap.py 590-600): a JSON-number account number is never scanned.
**What P5c as planned adds:** no pattern (plan-v3 81, 110: a redactor kind with a detector-model outline). This design does not close the gap either for numbers the run does not know (the check protects only known values, and only of 12+ raw characters for identifier kinds). **What this design asks P5c to add (PL-LK-7):** (i) PHONE's end changed from `(?![\w.])` to `(?!\w|\.\w)`, so a number at the end of a sentence is masked while `<digits>.5`, `<digits>.txt` and two runs joined by a full stop are not (round-2 probe "phone"; the repository suite was NOT run with it: UNVERIFIED); (ii) the strong account keywords ("account no/number/#", "acct", "A/C") count as bank context, so "Account No: <6-17 digits>" is masked with no bank word (false-mask rate UNVERIFIED, measured by P5c's evidence). **Not closed in v1, with the reason:** 8-9 or 17+ digits with no keyword (a context-free pattern of that length masks dates such as 20261105, amounts and invoice numbers); digits glued to letters; JSON numbers.
**Acceptance rows:** ACC-1a 8-9 digits, no keyword (xfail, named record); ACC-1b 17+ digits, no keyword (xfail); **ACC-1c a 10-16 digit number at the end of a sentence (must be masked; fails on `748849c`; P5c)**; **ACC-2 "Account No:" / "acct" / "A/C" + 8 digits, no bank word (must be masked; P5c)**; ACC-3 a JSON number (xfail; LKV-8).
