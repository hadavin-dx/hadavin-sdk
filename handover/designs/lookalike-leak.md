## 1. The question, in plain words
A value V that the redactor already knows is replaced by a placeholder when written plainly. The same V written with look-alike letters, other case, one combining mark, zero-width characters inside, full-width forms, or split over two lines goes to the model unchanged. What is the smallest design that makes such a copy impossible to send?
**One answer.** Any copy WITHIN ONE TEXT (or within one `redact_many` call) that a fold of the text still reads as V makes the redactor REFUSE the whole text (the check, section 5). A copy split across two separate texts (its head in one tool result, its tail in another) is not read (section 10; ADV-LK6-9). Draft 5's second answer, a case pass that masked other-case copies of stored names and addresses in place, stays DELETED (round 5; order 8).
**What "a folded copy" means in this note (exactly):** a text one of whose scan keys contains a scan key of V. The scan key is `scan_keyset` of P1b as P1 revision 11 defines it (item "P1b: identity, the readings, the keys") LESS its entity step, which revision 12 cuts (lead order 14(1); `scan_keyset` decodes nothing): tag texts T0-T3, NFKD and the confusables table, case folded in two readings, class maps `z` to `3` and `l` to `i` after the casefold, then every character for which `str.isalnum()` is False removed (whitespace, punctuation, symbols); the same steps run on V and on the text. The text is read as written AND after `runmap.undo` (JSON, backslash, percent and entity layers decoded), each read with the count rule of section 5 item 3. Anything a reader can see as V only through a transformation outside these functions is NOT covered (section 10).
**Scope, stated plainly.** (a) This closes the table for the core repository (`hadavin_sdk`). Warden's own `redaction.redact` is not fixed in the Warden repository (lead order 13(2)): when Warden is promoted, its redaction module is REPLACED by the core one (the retire list), so the fix for Warden is this check; section 8 gives the retire-list line and the Warden handover ticket text. (b) Stored NAME and ADDRESS values follow the evidence rule of lead order 13(1) (section 7, precondition P-1): if benign-document refusals with NAME and ADDRESS checked are at or below 1 in 200 texts, they are IN the v1 check; if above, they stay out, and the gap is one CHANGELOG line plus a row in the lead's later-fixes list. Two outcomes only, IN and OUT, as order 13(1) says (draft 6's middle step IN-GATED is deleted, ADV-LK6-6, COR-LK6-3). Until P-1 runs, which of the two P2d-2 builds is NOT decided; the code is built after P-1, for the outcome P-1 gives. (c) Every other known value (secrets and identifiers) is covered on every path in the table when it has a raw length of 12 or more AND at least one of its scan keys has 8 or more letters and digits (its shorter keys are dropped, section 5 item 1; COR-LK7-5). This is NARROWER than order 11's "12+ characters": a 12-character value of which 5 or more characters are not letters or digits (a password made mostly of punctuation) is not covered (ADV-LK6-4; PL-LK-36, ACCEPTED by lead order 14(2)). **Said plainly (lead order 14(2), with the correction of PL-LK-42):** copies of secrets of 8 to 11 characters (common passwords) are NOT caught when written with look-alike letters, and are NOT caught either when written in upper or lower case, with a combining mark or split over two lines (run on the snapshot, round-7 resolve fact 5); only their PLAIN copies are masked on every path, and their full-width or zero-width copies on the `redact_text` paths. They are never described as covered. (d) **Encoded copies (lead order 14(1)):** a known value encoded in invisible characters (bits written as zero-width characters or variation selectors), in base64 or in hex is caught by nothing in v1, whatever its length (section 10; strict xfail LK-ENC; the lead logs it as a v1.1 item with the trigger "before any pilot with real client data").

## 3. Three designs compared
**(a) Span-mask.** Fold, find V's key, map the hit back to written positions, replace only that span. Needs a position map; `_aligned_fold` (redact.py 1082) gives one only for simple folds and returns None otherwise (COR-R4-4), and then the line is withheld anyway. P2d-1's alias index is a smaller version of this idea and drew findings in rounds 3 to 5 (ADV-R3-2, ADV-R3-3, ADV-R3-8, ADV-R5-5, ADV-R5-6). Best fidelity, largest surface. Rejected for v1 (v1.1 LKV-1).
**(b) Refuse the whole text.** After all masking, if a scan key of V occurs in a scan key of the text, refuse. No position map. A person looks.
**(c) The round-4 fallback (`deny_hit` over the final output).** Gone: `deny_hit`, `prepare_needles` and `id_keyset` are cut from P1 v1 (P1 rev 11 and 11, PENDING LEAD 27). Its useful part, the scan key, is `scan_keyset`, which (b) calls directly. GOLD-1 is an independent leak oracle that shares none of the check's reading rules (section 6).
**Pick: (b), and nothing else.** Why: every table row is closed by an `in`-style check on folded keys; the failure is a refusal, not a leak (fail closed); the span machinery that kept failing review is not needed. What it costs: the whole text is lost when it holds a variant of a known value, including an upper-case copy of a known secret. **On the agent-loop paths a refusal ends the run**: the document path reports `redaction_error` (agent_loop 376-377; only `InputTooLarge` is reported as `document_too_large`, 371-375), tool results 469-470, tool inputs 495-496, the proposal check 649-650, all through `_redaction_failed` (547-549: a `guard/redaction failed` note and an abstention). **On the degrade paths it becomes a fixed text**: `audit/capped.py` 72-73 (withheld marker), `tools.error_reason` and `safe_head` (`SAFE_HEAD_NOTE`), and the approval request summary (its existing fallback card, section 5). **The k1b case bundle (an eval replay, not an agent run):** a refused string aborts the whole bundle, as any `RedactionError` other than `TooDeep` does today (`k1b/redaction.py` 189 catches `TooDeep` only; `k1b/replay.py` 158 has no handler; the replay's `except BaseException` at 273 keeps the output folder from looking finished); nothing of that bundle reaches the model (draft 6's edit there is CUT, ADV-LK6-2).
Size, honestly (an ESTIMATE): about 50 new lines in `redact.py` and `runmap.py` (one key memo with the needle forms, the check with its decoded form and the count rule, a plain substring search with deadline checks, the joined `redact_many` check and its length cap, the extra originals, the exception), one keyword parameter through `redact_text`, `redact_bounded`, `redact_untrusted`, `redact_layered` and `prepare_output`, one private keyword `_lookalike` on bare `redact`, 2 or 3 constants; and, in its own pull request P2d-2b, about 5 lines in `error_reason` and one argument at `runtime/agent_loop.py` 298. ## 4. Why a plain fold is not enough (decides the dependency)
My draft-1 prototype used the snapshot `fold` with case folding and all whitespace removed. All table rows were caught, but the table reads some look-alikes as a different character: Cyrillic Ze (U+0437) reads `3`, so a V holding `z` written with Ze is missed; Cyrillic Be (U+0431) reads `6`; small Greek eta (U+03B7) reads `n`. Confirmed in `_confusables.py`. P1b's class map (`z` to `3`) and two case readings close Ze and capital Eta. **So the check needs `scan_keyset`, and P2d-2 FOLLOWS P1b** (order 11; P1 rev 11 "What downstream packages wait for"). P1 keeps no class map for digit-shaped letters (PENDING LEAD 29, narrowed by PENDING LEAD 36 to digits): a known value that holds the DIGIT and is written with such a source is found; one that holds the LETTER and is written with a digit-shaped source (Be for `b`) is a leetspeak rewrite, not a look-alike, and stays open (LKV-3, xfail LK-BE). Class maps live in P1b only; this design adds none.
**What P1 revision 11 changes here (written; read by grep for this draft, COR-LK6-2):** the scan keys keep letters and digits only (`str.isalnum()`, so whitespace, punctuation, symbols, `?` and U+FFFD go) and map `l` to `i` after the casefold, on V and on the text alike. So dots, hyphens, underscores, `?` and U+FFFD between the letters of V are removed from the key, and capital I (or Cyrillic U+0406) written for small l reads the same: those copies are REFUSED (rows LK-FILL, LK-IL, LK-SURR, marked (rev 11)), and the key-length rule of section 5 counts after that removal. Revision 11 also says what is not folded: plain-ASCII digit look-alikes (`1` for `l`, `0` for `O`) and a letter in neither the Unicode data nor the generator's supplement (its "Stated limits" (11), row U+0138). `has_hidden_channel` keeps rules 1 and 2 only and both tiers are cut (PENDING LEAD 35, narrowed by the P1 round 10 resolve, PENDING LEAD 40; lead order 14(1)); this design never relied on rules 3 to 7 or on a tier. Revision 12 (lead order 14(1); not read, being written) also cuts the entity step of `scan_keyset`; rows LK-FILL, LK-IL, LK-SURR rest on revision 11's line 58, which the round-10 resolve keeps (UNVERIFIED against revision 12's text).
**A class of letters the table drops (ADV-LK6-3; found and counted by this draft on the snapshot data).** The generator keeps a UTS #39 "MA" entry only when its target is entirely ASCII letters and digits (`tools/gen_confusables.py`, selection rules). The data gives many Latin letters with a stroke or bar as an ASCII letter PLUS a combining overlay, for example U+0127 `ħ` as `h` + U+0335, U+0167 `ŧ`, U+0180 `ƀ`, U+01B6 `ƶ`, U+0268 `ɨ`, U+0126 `Ħ` likewise; NFKD leaves them unchanged; so none of them is in the 677-entry table (only the supplement's U+00F8, U+0142, U+0111 are). Count on the snapshot data: 177 MA entries (148 of them letters) whose target, with its combining marks removed, is ASCII letters and digits, all dropped. U+0289 `ʉ` has no MA entry at all. A copy of V with `ħ` for `h` is therefore SENT today and under P1 revision 11 as written. The fix belongs to P1's generator (one selection rule: test the target after removing Mn and Me characters, plus a property test over the data; section 8); until P1 takes it, row LK-STROKE is a strict xfail here (it flips to refused, like LK-IL, when P1 does).
From P1 revision 12, P1 supplies no decoder at all (lead order 14(1): the entity step of `scan_keyset` is cut; one decode path, `runmap.undo` beside each text, instead of two that disagree). Every decoded form therefore comes from this design, through the existing `runmap.undo` (runmap.py 139-170), on BOTH sides: each checked text is read as written and as `undo(text)` (section 5 item 3), and each checked original as written and, when different, as `undo(original)` (section 5 item 1; new in draft 8: a stored value holding entity-like text such as `x&notY`, shown decoded as `x` + the `¬` sign + `Y`, was caught by the entity step on both sides and would be lost by the cut, P1 round 10 honest note (c); row LK-ENT-VAL). **What `undo`'s fixed point loses (RES-R10-3, relayed by the P1 round 10 resolve, its fact 2; read, not run for this draft):** `undo` repeats its decoders until nothing changes, so a `&` standing right before a value whose letter is written as a numeric entity can join the decoded letters into one of the 106 entity names that `html.unescape` decodes without a semicolon: `&&#108;tq-...` gives `&ltq-...` in round 1 and `<q-...` in round 2, so a known value starting with `ltq` loses `lt` in the decoded form, while the written form reads the digits `108`. Neither form finds that copy: strict xfail LK-ENT-AMP, LKV-15. Reading every `undo` round as its own form would close it, but costs one `scan_keyset` call per round (about 2 s each at the size cap, UNVERIFIED), which does not fit the 6.0 s budget; stated, not built (PL-LK-46).
The design uses no other P1 function: `squeeze` and `fold` are not called; P1 keeps both public for their own callers (PENDING LEAD 37).

## 5. The design (b), exactly
**New public function** `check_lookalike(texts, mapping, *, extra=(), _cache=None, _deadline=None) -> None` in `guard/redact.py`; it raises **`LookalikeCopy`**, a new public subclass of `runmap.RedactionError` defined in `runmap.py` and raised from `redact.py` through a function-level import (the precedent: `_Redactor._over_budget`, redact.py 986-992, raises `InputTooLarge` the same way; `runmap.undo` is imported the same way). Message = class name only. Bare callers that catch `RedactionError` catch it. **Every textnorm call of the check (`scan_keyset` on each original, on each text and on its decoded form) is made in the body of `check_lookalike` itself, and its only exception handler, which names `TextnormError` and counts a hit, is there too**: `check_lookalike` is one of the THREE v1 entries of P1's `REFUSAL_HANDLERS` (P1 rev 11, "the caller rule and the refusal lint": `guard/redact.py::check_lookalike`, the P2d-2 withhold function, the P5a mapper); no helper and no memo function calls textnorm (ADV-LK5-8). The search (item 2) is plain `in` in the same body and calls no textnorm function. There is no `except Exception` around a textnorm call (lint (a) has no exemption; COR-R8-9).
1. **Values and keys.** Every original of `mapping` except `"\0seed"` entries (round-5 resolve: kept; FR-13 now has an input that kills its removal) and except, in outcome OUT of P-1, originals whose kind is in `defaults.LOOKALIKE_WORD_KINDS = ("NAME", "ADDRESS")` (below), plus every string in `extra` (treated as a secret; never added to the map). There is no list of included kinds: a kind added to the redactor later is checked by default (ADV-LK4-9). **One length rule for every kind:** the RAW original has at least `defaults.LOOKALIKE_MIN_RAW_CHARS = 12` characters (`len(original)`, spaces counted) AND at least one key of `scan_keyset(original)` has `defaults.LOOKALIKE_MIN_KEY_CHARS = 8` or more characters, counted after the key's removals; each key under 8 is dropped (COR-LK7-5). **Needle forms (new in draft 8):** when `runmap.undo(original)` differs from the original, its keys are needles too, under the same rules (row LK-ENT-VAL, mutant M53); a `TooDeep` from `undo(original)` counts like an unfoldable original (a hit, below). So a secret of 12 or more characters, a 36-character UUID, a 14-character id written with spaces are covered; an 11-character SSN-shaped value, a 10-character PAN, any secret of 8 to 11 characters, and a 12-character secret of which 5 or more characters are not letters or digits are not (xfail LK-SHORT; FR-6b; LKV-2; said in the owner summary, ADV-LK5-13). Keys are built once per original and kept in a per-run memo `cache["lookalike_keys"]` (original -> its keys, or the marker "unfoldable"; in the run's cache, never module-level, R371). **An original that `scan_keyset` refuses (`TextnormError`) counts as a hit: the check raises `LookalikeCopy` for every checked text while that value is in the map** (fail closed; P1 error contract, ADV-R8-5, PENDING LEAD 34; row LK-UNFOLD). **"The map" is `current`, the map as this call has grown it (the values the patterns found in this very text included), not the `mapping` the caller handed in** (mutant M48). Consequence (ADV-LK6-8, read): a value FOUND in a text and refused by P1's fold refuses that same text, and it is never stored, because `redact_text` updates the caller's map only after a pass (runmap.py 526-527) and bare `redact` works on a copy of the map (redact.py 953) that it returns only on success. So a planted unfoldable value cannot make every later check of the run fail; only a value that a caller puts into the map itself (the MAP way) can, and then every check while it is held refuses (stated, section 10).
   **Word kinds (NAME, ADDRESS): built for the outcome of P-1 (section 7; lead order 13(1), two outcomes).** Outcome IN: no exclusion, `LOOKALIKE_WORD_KINDS` is not added, word kinds are checked like every other kind. Outcome OUT: word-kind originals are skipped (`LOOKALIKE_WORD_KINDS = ("NAME", "ADDRESS")`). Effect: IN closes every table row for names and addresses and refuses benign texts the way P-1 measured; OUT sends every variant copy of a stored name or address (rows LK-WORD, LK-WORD-ASCII). ## 7. False-refusal rate and cost: what is and is not known, and two preconditions
Not measured on real documents; no figure is guessed. Known: (1) after masking, a PLAIN copy of V is normally a placeholder. The sweep leaves plain copies in three production cases (an identifier inside a longer ASCII letter/digit run, words in `_COMMON`, values under 3 characters or all-digit under 6); only the first produces refusals (`_COMMON` words are at most 10 characters and short values are under the minimum). (2) Chance matches are negligible for random secrets and ids: the draft-1 prototype found 0 hits for 8,000 random 12-40-character keys in 262,144 characters; by arithmetic, a random 8-character key matches about 262,144 / 36^8 = 9e-8 per text per value, a 12-digit number about 2.6e-7 (revision 11's `l` to `i` map makes the alphabet 35, a change below the precision of these figures). (3) Word kinds: their refusal cost depends on the P-1 outcome (FR-17, FR-24, FR-29). (4) The named risks that remain in every outcome: other-case copies of secrets and of case-sensitive identifiers (LK-UP, FR-30), identifiers glued to letters or digits (FR-11, FR-23), long quoted captures (FR-19), plain copies of a stored value that holds a pattern-shaped inner value (FR-38), the count form of texts full of placeholders (FR-35), Mn-only differences (FR-5), an unfoldable stored value (LK-UNFOLD), benign near-cap texts whose expansion is over the fold limit (FR-33), and the wider class rules of P1 revision 11 (every non-alphanumeric removed, `l` read as `i`).
**Precondition P-1 (false refusals; ADV-LK4-11; lead order 13(1)).** After P1b merges and before P2d-2 is built (the lead's `S0 EXIT` for P2d-2 waits for it), an evidence runner runs a throwaway prototype of the check on P1b's `scan_keyset` (not repository code) over a made-up corpus of **400 texts**: 200 invoices and letters (English ones with typographic quotes, dashes and currency signs; German and French ones with accented letters; some address blocks in capitals), 100 tool results (ERP/database style with padded columns, one with ANSI colour, one Word/PDF-style extract with private-use bullets, ADV-R9-5 of the P1 round 9), 100 model tool-call arguments; plus near-cap texts in Korean, Vietnamese and Arabic (ADV-LK5-12). Stored names, addresses, user names, tenant ids, cloud resource ids and passwords of 8 to 16 characters, some dictionary words, written plain, in capitals, padded, re-broken and accented.
