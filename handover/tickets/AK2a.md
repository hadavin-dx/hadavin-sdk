**Words used (plain meaning, one meaning each).**
- **document**: one file a client gives us (a PDF or a plain-text file). **page**: one page of it, as the reader returns it. **reader**: the part that turns the file's bytes into page texts. **text PDF**: a PDF whose pages carry real text; a **scan** is a PDF whose pages are pictures of text.
- **quality report**: numbers about one document (pages, characters per page, share of characters that are not text, counts of invisible characters, whether it looks like a scan) plus one **quality code** from a closed list. It never holds document text. It names neither the language nor the writing system (row 18 of the resolve cut that; row V3).
- **cleaning**: a fixed list of small text repairs (line endings, control characters, words split by a line-end hyphen, repeated page headers and footers, page-number lines, extra spaces). **removal report**: for each repair, how many characters it removed. Never text.
- **chunk** (passage): one piece of the cleaned document, cut at headings, paragraphs and sentences, with its page range and position. **chunker**: the part that cuts. **chunk set**: all chunks of one document plus the two reports.
- **child process**: a separate Python process the PDF reader starts for each PDF, so a hostile or broken file cannot crash, hang or fill the memory of our own process. **parent**: our own process, which starts the child.
- **tag characters**: the invisible Unicode characters U+E0000 to U+E007F, which can spell hidden words that a person does not see.
- **step**, **item**, **final state** (`done`, `needs_person` = "a person must look", `refused`), **tenant**, **readers** (the people allowed to read an item), **gate**, **`Gated`** (the one wrapper the gate gives code instead of a raw outbound part): as in AK1 revision 2 (`tickets/AK1.md`, "Words used", `:16`).

AK2a adds a reader that reports how well each document was read and sends poor or broken documents to a person, a cleaning step that says exactly what it removed, a chunker that follows headings and sentences, and a child process that contains a bad PDF. Readers commonly accept bytes before the PDF header; AK2a looks in the first 1,024 bytes and fails closed (the ISO 32000 wording is not relied on). Stated limit: a file whose `%PDF-` sits after byte 1,024 is read as text, like any text file holding PDF syntax.
  5. **Cleaning (AK2a-1, `ingest/clean.py`).** `clean(pages: tuple[Page, ...], settings) -> tuple[tuple[Page, ...], RemovalReport, bool]` (the `bool` is "over-cleaned"). `CLEAN_RULES: Final`, closed; ids unchanged from revision 1, **applied in this order: c1, c2, c6, c4, c5, c3, c7** (trailing spaces go before the join, and the join runs after footers are removed; resolve fact 4):
     - `c1_newlines`: `\r\n` and lone `\r` to `\n` (counts removed `\r`).
     - `c2_controls`: removes C0 controls except `\t` and `\n`, DEL, and C1 controls (U+0080-U+009F). Does NOT touch format characters (category Cf: zero-width, bidirectional marks, tag characters), combining marks or U+FFFD (I7).
     - `c6_spaces`: runs of spaces and tabs inside a line become one space; trailing spaces removed.
     - `c4_running_lines`: a line that is the first or last non-blank line of at least `KIT_RUNNING_LINE_MIN_PAGES` (3) pages AND of at least `KIT_RUNNING_LINE_MIN_SHARE_PPM` (500,000 = half) of all pages, compared after digits are replaced by `#` and spaces collapsed, is removed from every page where it is the first or last line.
     - `c5_page_numbers`: only on documents of 2 pages or more, a first or last line that is only a page number is removed when the number equals the page's position (1-based) or the position minus 1 (one unnumbered cover page), in the forms `12`, `- 12 -`, `page 12` (any case), or is `n/m` or `page n of m` with `m` equal to the page count. Any other number line (`2024`, `42` on page 3) is kept.
     - `c3_hyphen_join`: a letter-hyphen-newline-letter sequence where both letters are lower case (`str.islower`) loses the hyphen and the newline. Upper case or digits on either side: kept. Stated limit: a real compound split at a line end (`"well-\nknown"`) also loses its hyphen.
     - `c7_blank_lines`: three or more newlines become two.
     - **Passes:** `clean` runs the whole list up to `KIT_CLEAN_MAX_PASSES` (3) times and stops at the first pass that removes nothing. If pass 3 still removes something, the result is over-cleaned. Each pass is linear in the text. So idempotence holds by construction for every result that is not over-cleaned.
     - **Over-cleaning:** over-cleaned also when `sum(removed) > KIT_MAX_REMOVED_SHARE_PPM` (300,000 = 30 percent) of `chars_before`. The step maps it to `over_cleaned`. **Stated cost:** c4 peels one repeated line per page edge per pass, so a document with three or more stacked repeated header lines (or footer lines) still changes on pass 3 and goes to a person (pinned in R11; measured on a client sample in V6; the setting can be raised).
  6. **Chunking (AK2a-1, `ingest/chunk.py`).** `Chunker` (Protocol): `name: str`, `chunk(pages, settings, *, tenant, item_id, content_sha256, reader) -> tuple[Chunk, ...]`. One implementation in v1, `StructureChunker` (`name = "structure-1"`); the blank-line alternative of revision 1 is cut (row V15).
     - **Text the chunker works on** (`joined`): the cleaned pages joined with `"\n\n"`; `char_start`/`char_end` index into it; `page_start`/`page_end` are the pages of the first and last character.
     - **Blocks and headings.** Blocks = text between blank lines. A **heading** is the FIRST line of a block when that line is at most `KIT_HEADING_MAX_CHARS` (120) characters, does not end in `.`, `,` or `;`, and is either (a) numbered (`1`, `1.2`, `1.2.3` up to `KIT_MAX_HEADING_DEPTH` (8) parts, or a Roman numeral or a single capital letter followed by `.` or `)`, then a space and text), (b) a Markdown heading (`#` to `######` then a space), or (c) at least 3 letters with every letter upper case. The rest of the block is the section's first paragraph. A line in the middle of a block is never a heading (numbered list items inside a paragraph stay paragraph text). Level: the number of numbered parts, the `#` count, or 1 for (c). A heading closes every open section of its level or deeper and starts a new chunk; the heading line stays in the text of the first chunk of its section (so coverage holds) and is also in `heading_path` (outermost first, at most `KIT_MAX_HEADING_DEPTH` entries; a deeper "heading" is a paragraph).
     - **Packing and cuts.** Blocks of one section are packed into a chunk until adding the next would pass `KIT_CHUNK_MAX_CHARS` (2,000). A longer block is split at the last sentence end (`.`, `?`, `!`, `;` followed by whitespace) before the limit, else the last whitespace, else a hard cut at the limit; no cut falls between a character and a following combining mark (category M), a ZERO WIDTH JOINER or a variation selector. A chunk shorter than `KIT_CHUNK_MIN_CHARS` (200) merges into the next chunk of the same section only if the result stays at or under `KIT_CHUNK_MAX_CHARS`; otherwise it stays short (the size cap wins). The sentence and whitespace searches look only inside one chunk window, so the work is at most `len(joined) x (KIT_CHUNK_MAX_CHARS + KIT_CHUNK_OVERLAP_CHARS)`.
     - **Overlap**: each chunk after the first in a section starts with up to `KIT_CHUNK_OVERLAP_CHARS` (150) characters of the previous chunk's end, moved forward to a whitespace boundary; never across a heading (0 there); `overlap_chars` says how many. Overlap repeats text, so a personal value near a cut appears in two chunks; the gate masks each.
     - **Coverage rule** (K5): for every chunk `text == joined[char_start:char_end]`; dropping each chunk's first `overlap_chars` characters and joining the rest, separated by the block gaps the cut fell in, reproduces `joined` without losing or repeating a non-space character.
     - **Chunk ids**: `chunk_id(...) = "chk-" + sha256_hex(tenant_key(tenant, content_sha256, settings_fp, chunker_name, reader, str(index), sha256_hex(text)))[:32]` (AK1's length-prefixed `tenant_key`, each part a `str` of 1 to 512 characters, `tickets/AK1.md:92`; the `prefix-<32 hex>` shape of `models.py:23-24`, but derived, not random). Same bytes, tenant, settings, chunker and library version give the same ids (AK2d needs that); another tenant, another chunker name, another library version (it is in `reader`) or another chunk text gives another id. `chunk_id` is a module function so K8 and `from_bytes` call it directly.
     - **Injection flag**: per chunk, `injection_flag = injection_check(detector, chunk.text, source="kit_ingest").flagged OR (the chunk holds any tag character U+E0000-U+E007F)`. The text is never changed (I7). Stated plainly: a phrase split by zero-width characters is caught only for the three squashed phrases (`guard/injection.py:35`, `:44-48`); a phrase cut at a heading boundary (where there is no overlap) is caught only if one side alone matches; tag-spelled words are not read, only flagged.
  7. **The step (AK2a-1, `ingest/step.py`).** `DocumentStep(readers: Mapping[str, DocumentReader], chunker: Chunker, detector: Detector, settings: IngestSettings)`, `name = "read_and_chunk"`, implements AK1-a's `Step`.      - (b) `len(inp.body) > KIT_MAX_FILE_BYTES` (32 MiB) gives `too_large` BEFORE any reader is called.
     - (c) `pick_reader`; `None` gives `unsupported_type`.

     - (e) `ingest.quality.assess(pages, settings) -> QualityReport`, first failure wins: all pages have zero non-space characters -> `empty`; `nontext_share_ppm > KIT_MAX_NONTEXT_PPM` (50,000 = 5 percent) -> `high_nontext`, where the numerator counts U+FFFD, private-use (Co), unassigned (Cn), surrogate (Cs) and control characters other than `\t`, `\n`, `\r` and `\f` (line and page structure that c1 and the reader remove), and the denominator is all characters of all raw pages, spaces included; then, looking ONLY at pages whose `has_images` is `True` or `None` (a page with `has_images=False`, which every `TextReader` page is, can trip only `empty`, `high_nontext` and `over_cleaned`): poor pages over `KIT_MAX_POOR_PAGE_PPM` (100,000 = 10 percent) of all pages -> `suspected_scan`, where a **poor page** has fewer than `KIT_MIN_CHARS_PER_PAGE` (100) non-space characters (`None` counts as poor: fail closed); median non-space characters over those pages below `KIT_MIN_CHARS_PER_PAGE` -> `low_text`.      - **Mapping to AK1-a outcomes** (`STEP_OUTCOME: Final[Mapping[str, tuple[Outcome, str | None]]]`, one row per code, total over `QUALITY_CODES`, row R13): `ok` -> `done`; the 5 poor codes -> `needs_person` `low_quality`; the 10 unreadable codes -> `needs_person` `UNREADABLE_REASON`.      - **The quality code reaches a person:** AK1's outcome audit event carries the reason only, so on every non-`done` output `DocumentStep` also writes one INFO log record `ingest_outcome` with `extra={"item_id": ..., "quality_code": ...}` (ids and codes only, never text).      - Exceptions: AK2a raises none for a bad document; an unexpected bug raises and AK1-a's runner turns it into `needs_person step_error` (`tickets/AK1.md:99`).
