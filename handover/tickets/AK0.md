## What AK0 is, in one paragraph
AK0 is a written note, `handover/ak0-decisions.md`, plus a folder of dated source notes, `handover/evidence/AK0/`. It writes down what the core already has for the Assembly Kit's document-question parts (RAG: answering questions from a client's documents). For each part it lists the ready-made options on the tool ladder, with licence, cost and risk, and records one decision per part with the evidence behind it. The decisions that only the owner can take go to the lead with the numbers. Every later kit ticket (AK1 to AK4b) reads its defaults from this note instead of re-arguing them. AK0 builds nothing, installs nothing, signs up for nothing, downloads nothing and spends nothing; it makes no model or embedding call (order 27 item 55).
