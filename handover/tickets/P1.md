## Words used in this ticket (defined once)
- **fold**: the function that turns text into a comparison form in which look-alike characters, invisible characters and case no longer matter. **key**: the folded text (a string) a view returns. A key can hold personal data; never log it. **table_digest**: the SHA-256 of the look-alike table (not of a key).
- **view**: one named function that returns a key or a key-set for one purpose. The four view names are exactly `identity_key`, `id_key`, `scan_keyset`, `id_keyset` (the constant `VIEWS`); no other spelling is used anywhere (`names_equal` is a prose helper, not a view).
- **DENY decision**: "block this if it looks like a protected value" (blocklists, leak scans, same person); over-matching is the safe side. **ALLOW decision**: "accept only if it IS the verified value"; the fold is never used for it; `allow_equal` is the only allow path.
- **reading**: one complete way of turning a text into a key. A **key-set** is the small set of keys one text gets (one per reading); two texts match when their key-sets share a key. **FOLD_VERSION**: ONE string naming exactly which fold produced a key (P1a).
- **hidden channel**: a set of characters that show nothing (or almost nothing) and can carry text or bits. Exactly (defined once, used everywhere): (a) the tag block U+E0000 to U+E007F; (b) every character with the Unicode property `Variation_Selector`: U+180B to U+180D, U+180F, U+FE00 to U+FE0F, U+E0100 to U+E01EF (the property set, not a hand-written pair of ranges: round-4 finding A1); (c) hidden characters of two tiers, decided by `_hidden_tier(ch)` (defined exactly under Interface > Signals): **tier P** (27 format characters that real prose uses, such as the zero-width non-joiner in Persian) counts only as a run of 8 or more, or as more than 64 characters AND more than 10 percent of the text; **tier N** (every other character `fold` removes for being a format, control, private-use, lone-surrogate or unassigned character, except whitespace controls) counts when more than 4 of them stand anywhere in one text. Revision 7's single flat "invisible set" of 141,349 members is gone (round-6 findings ADV-R6-1 and ADV-R6-2). The two emoji selectors U+FE0E and U+FE0F are a special case (rule in Interface, `has_hidden_channel`). **refuse**: the caller treats the text as blocked.

## Not in v1: P1c, stored keys (outline only; lead order 7(3))
Ten lines, no interface, no tests, no mutants, no errors in this ticket.
1. Purpose: let a package STORE a fold key and compare it later across a fold change.
2. Why not now: nothing in v1 stores a key; the Interim rule (store raw text, re-fold with the current `identity_key`) costs nothing; a stored key freezes the algorithm.
3. Trigger: the first package that must store a fold key because raw text may not be kept (P3b; P2a if it stores names).
4. It would need: a stored form that carries the fold version, a parse that refuses a different version loudly, and a salted keyed digest for personal values (never a bare digest).
5. It would need a decision on who migrates stored keys when `alg` changes and who owns that cost (the number D above).
6. Not built: `view_version`, `parse_stored_form`, a `p` marker in the version string, stored-form errors.
7. The version string is `alg|tbl|ucd|ud` since revision 8 (the `beh` field was cut; its v1.1 row names P1c as the trigger that brings it back, because a stored key must be able to tell that a constant changed) with no field reserved for the stored-form marker; adding a field later changes `FOLD_VERSION` once, on purpose.
8. Review class would be Tier A (redaction and personal data).
9. Estimated size: a smaller pull request than P1b; not estimated further.

## Which view each caller uses, and the stated limits
- **Blocklist and leak scan (DENY):** `prepare_needles` once, then `deny_hit` per text (scan view for words and names; id view for invoice numbers, account numbers and similar ids; a digit-only needle of 6 or 7 characters can be loaded in the scan view only, because the id-view minimum is 8; see `prepare_needles`). The caller treats `True` as "block". DENY comparisons use `scan_keyset` and `id_keyset` only, never `id_key` equality (ADV-R5-12).
- **Same person (DENY):** `same_person`. **ALLOW (accept only the verified value):** `allow_equal` only; never a key.
- **Lines of a document:** `line_view` (keeps the line structure). **Display or storage:** raw text; no key is stored (Interim rule).
- **Stated limits (not hidden):** (1) the scan view over-matches on purpose (`mark` is found in `tomark us` and in `market`; `Anna Maria` equals `Ann Amaria`); (2) the pass A/B case limit: a text that mixes capital and small Greek letters inside ONE word may be read one way only (`scan_match("ΗΗ","hn")` is False); (3) JSON, percent and `\x` escapes are not decoded; (4) digit-only needles are chance-matched in long numeric text: at 6 digits **about 4 % of random 50,000-digit tables and about 20 % of random 250,000-digit tables** hit one needle (prototype: 17 of 400 and 41 of 200; revision 6 wrote 8 % for the second, which was wrong; the evidence runner re-measures), at 8 digits 0 of 600 (prototype, small sample); (5) a hidden-channel haystack is refused by default, so a document with a long run of zero-width characters is blocked, never read; a document that contains an England, Scotland or Wales flag emoji is blocked too (rule 1 refuses the whole tag block), and so is a text with more than 4 unassigned, private-use or control characters (tier N), such as an icon-font line; (6) no cache; (7) time is bounded by the budget, not by a promise per call; (8) the hidden-channel rules do not close every channel: up to 512 variation selectors (about 1.2 KB), a tier P channel below the run and share limits (about 31 KB with positions at the cap), at most 4 tier N characters (about 18 bytes), and channels made of combining marks or of whitespace characters are neither refused nor decoded (arithmetic and v1.1 rows under Signals).
