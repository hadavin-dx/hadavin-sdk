## Words used in this ticket (defined once)
- **fold**: the function that turns text into a comparison form in which look-alike characters, invisible characters and case no longer matter. **key**: the folded text (a string) a view returns. A key can hold personal data; never log it. **table_digest**: the SHA-256 of the look-alike table (not of a key).
- **view**: one named function that returns a key or a key-set for one purpose. The two view names are exactly `identity_key` (P1a) and `scan_keyset` (P1b); `id_key`, `line_view` and the constant `VIEWS` are cut to v1.1 (ADV-R8-6, ADV-R8-7) and `id_keyset` was cut earlier (ADV-R7-6); no other spelling is used anywhere (`names_equal` is a prose helper, not a view).
- **DENY decision**: "block this if it looks like a protected value" (blocklists, leak scans, same person); over-matching is the safe side. **ALLOW decision**: "accept only if it IS the verified value"; the fold is never used for it; `allow_equal` is the only allow path.
- **reading**: one complete way of turning a text into a key. A **key-set** is the small set of keys one text gets (one per reading); two texts match when their key-sets share a key. **FOLD_VERSION**: ONE string naming exactly which fold produced a key (P1a).
- **hidden channel**: a set of characters that show nothing (or almost nothing) and can carry text or bits. Exactly (defined once, used everywhere): (a) the tag block U+E0000 to U+E007F; (b) every character with the Unicode property `Variation_Selector`: U+180B to U+180D, U+180F, U+FE00 to U+FE0F, U+E0100 to U+E01EF (the property set, not a hand-written pair of ranges: round-4 finding A1); (c) hidden characters of two tiers, decided by `_hidden_tier(ch)` (defined exactly under Interface > Signals): **tier P** (27 format characters that real prose uses, such as the zero-width non-joiner in Persian) counts only as a run of 8 or more, or as more than 16 characters AND more than 10 percent of the text; **tier N** (every other character `fold` removes for being a format, control, private-use, lone-surrogate or unassigned character, except whitespace controls) counts when more than 1 of them stands anywhere in one text. Revision 7's single flat "invisible set" of 141,349 members is gone (round-6 findings ADV-R6-1 and ADV-R6-2). The two emoji selectors U+FE0E and U+FE0F are a special case (rule in Interface, `has_hidden_channel`). **No limit in this ticket protects a short secret from an encoded copy; these rules bound the COUNTED channels only; combining marks, whitespace characters and HTML-entity-encoded characters are NOT counted and are UNBOUNDED in v1 (ADV-R7-4, ADV-R8-4; the figures are under Signals).** **refuse**: the caller treats the text as blocked.

## v1.1 detail: the DENY needle API (cut from v1 in revision 9, ADV-R7-6; PENDING LEAD 27)
This section keeps the design of revisions 5 to 8 so that a later package can start from it. **Nothing here is in v1: no code, no test, no mutant and no constant of it is part of P1.** It was cut because no package ticket had a named production caller with its own test (P2d-2 calls `scan_keyset`; P5a names none of these functions), and because the surface (6 public names, 7 `TEXTNORM_` constants, 3 parameters) carried most of the review findings of rounds 5 to 7.
- **Functions (as designed):** `prepare_needles(needles_raw, *, view="scan_keyset", allow_empty=False) -> PreparedNeedles` at load time (the WHOLE list fails with `BadNeedle` on the first bad entry; an empty list is bad unless `allow_empty=True`); `needle_problem(raw, *, view) -> str | None` (the same checks on one entry; reason names `not_text`, `too_long`, `hidden`, `empty`, `too_short`, `too_short_digits`, `unfoldable`); `deny_hit(prepared, haystack_raw, *, refuse_hidden=True, clock=time.thread_time) -> bool` (True means REFUSE: on a match, on a hidden channel, on a `prepared` of the wrong type, on a passed deadline, on any `Exception`); `id_keyset(raw)` (per tag text T0 to T3, `squeeze(T, loose=True)` then the class map `3` to `2`, at most 4 keys); the private `_scan_contains`.
- **Order of the checks on one entry:** type and raw cap; the bounded entity step (so `&#xE0061;` becomes a tag character before the next checks); length cap again and `has_hidden_channel`; the key (empty is bad); the minimums on the FINAL key (after the entity step, the fold and the maps, never on the raw entry).
- **Per-view minimums (proposal):** scan view 6, id view 8, digit-only keys 6 (`TEXTNORM_MIN_NEEDLE_SCAN`, `TEXTNORM_MIN_NEEDLE_ID`, `TEXTNORM_MIN_NEEDLE_DIGITS`). **The digit minimum 6 is DECIDED (lead order 8(2)).** The digit rule changes nothing in the scan view (6 against 6) and is weaker than the per-view rule in the id view (6 against 8). Short names are compared by the caller whole-token with `same_person`.
- **Why the digit minimum (the chance-hit paragraph, moved here whole from revision 8):** Why a minimum at all: a short needle in a squashed haystack over-refuses. Prototype (revision 6, 64-bit integer generator, random digits, NOT a measurement of the final code and NOT re-run by the round-5 reviewers; the evidence runner re-measures it): a 6-digit needle was found in 17 of 400 trials in 50,000 random digits (**about 4 %**, 4.3 %; chance expected 1 - (1 - 10^-6)^50,000 = 4.9 %) and in 41 of 200 trials in 250,000 random digits (**about 20 %**, 20.5 %; chance expected 22 %). **Correction (ADV-R5-5, R5-C6): revision 6 wrote "about 8 %" for the 250,000-digit case, which understated the false-hit rate at that size by a factor of about 2.5 (8 against 20.5), and it was copied into the lead's decision text; the figures above replace every copy.** Revision 6 also wrote "expected 5 %" and "expected 25 %", a linear estimate; the exact chance is 4.9 % and 22 %. An 8-digit needle was found in 0 of 400 and 0 of 200 (chance 0.05 % and 0.25 %); a 4-digit needle in 99 of 100. So a 6-digit minimum for digit-only needles still over-refuses numeric tables: 500 such needles against a 250,000-digit table would refuse it almost surely (1 - 0.78^500). **Per needle (ADV-R6-11):** every chance figure above is for ONE needle; for N needles the chance of at least one hit is 1 - (1 - p)^N, so 500 needles of 6 digits against 250,000 random digits is almost sure. **Two positions, both written (Decision 5):** the round-5 resolve keeps 6 (a false hit refuses a document and a person looks: the safe direction); I recommended 8 in revision 6. I now state my disagreement plainly: with a minimum of 8 a 6-digit identifier in a blocklist makes `prepare_needles` fail LOUDLY at load time (the whole list, never a partial list), it is not silently unprotected, so the resolve's reason "8 would be a leak" does not hold as written; the real trade is a loud load failure for 6- and 7-digit identifiers against a false-refusal rate of about 20 % on very long numeric documents. **DECIDED (lead order 8(2)): the minimum is 6; the second position stays written as the record of the trade and is no longer an open point.**
- **Budget and clock:** the deadline `TEXTNORM_SCAN_BUDGET_S` (proposal 2.0 s) starts at entry of `deny_hit` and is checked after the entity step, after each key and between needles. The clock was `time.thread_time`, the clock `REDACT_BUDGET_S` is measured in (`runmap.py:484`, `started = time.thread_time()`, at `748849c`, re-read). Prototype: one search of the hostile pair (needle `"a"*63+"b"`, haystack `"a"` at the cap) took 1.16 ms, so 500 needles of that form take about 2.3 s per call with 4 searches each, above the 2.0 s budget (the deadline fires and `deny_hit` returns True); random keys took about 0.15 ms. For k lists scanned one after the other the rule was k x (budget + S) <= the caller's allowance; it is NOT in v1 (SK-4 deletes it).

## Which view each caller uses, and the stated limits
- **Blocklist and look-alike scan (DENY):** `scan_keyset(text)` per text and per known value, and the caller compares key-sets with substring search (the oracle for the look-alike design is `any(k in t for k in K for t in scan_keyset(T))`, GOLD-1); the contract is SK-1 to SK-6 under "P1b: `scan_keyset`". The caller applies its own minimum length (P2d-2: `LOOKALIKE_MIN_KEY_CHARS`) and treats any `TextnormError` as a hit or a refusal, in a function listed in `REFUSAL_HANDLERS`. The many-needle `deny_hit` and the id DENY view are cut to v1.1 (PENDING LEAD 27). The `id_key` equality check of ADV-R5-12 has nothing to refuse because `id_key` is cut; DENY comparisons use `scan_keyset` key-sets.
- **Same person (DENY):** `same_person`. **ALLOW (accept only the verified value):** `allow_equal` only; never a key.
- **Lines of a document:** no view in v1 (`line_view` is cut, PENDING LEAD 32). **Display or storage:** raw text; no key is stored (Interim rule).
- **Stated limits (not hidden):** (1) the scan view over-matches on purpose (`mark` is found in `tomark us` and in `market`; `Anna Maria` equals `Ann Amaria`); (2) the pass A/B case limit: a text that mixes capital and small Greek letters inside ONE word may be read one way only (`_scan_match("ΗΗ","hn")` is False); (3) JSON, percent and `\x` escapes are not decoded; (4) a caller that scans for short digit-only values is chance-matched in long numeric text (about 4 % of random 50,000-digit tables and about 20 % of random 250,000-digit tables hit one 6-digit needle; the figures are in the v1.1 detail section); (5) `has_hidden_channel` is TRUE for a long run of zero-width characters, for an England, Scotland or Wales flag emoji (rule 1 refuses the whole tag block) and for a text with 2 or more unassigned, private-use or control characters (tier N), such as an icon-font line or two ANSI colour codes; a caller decides what to do; (6) no cache; (7) the time of one `scan_keyset` call is a stated figure, S_call (SK-4), not a promise; (8) **no limit in this ticket protects a short secret from an encoded copy. These rules bound the COUNTED channels only. Of the channels this signal counts: after the limits, tier N alone carries about 4.7 bytes, the tier P total branch about 45 bytes, the selector rule about 1.8 KB and the tier P share branch about 15.6 KB (about 31 KB with positions; about 32.8 KB in all); a 12-character secret at 7 bits is about 10.5 bytes and fits the last three. Three channels are NOT counted and are UNBOUNDED: combining marks (Mn, Me; up to about 359 KB), whitespace characters (up to about 159 KB) and characters written as HTML entities (`has_hidden_channel` reads raw text).** They are neither refused nor decoded (arithmetic and v1.1 rows under Signals; PENDING LEAD 33); (9) **ASCII look-alikes** (`I`, `l`, `1`; `O`, `0`) are not supplied by the generated table and are not folded by `fold`; only `squeeze(loose=True)` reads them (design item for P2d-2, PENDING LEAD 34 relay); (10) **availability limit (ADV-R8-5):** nine nested `&amp;` give `UnresolvedEntities`, a `str` subclass gives `NotText`, an over-size text gives `TextTooLong` and one unfoldable stored value makes the check refuse the text; an attacker who controls a stored value can switch a check off in this way if the caller skips it, so P1 never skips and `check_lookalike` refuses (PENDING LEAD 34). Choices a reader can SEE (case, a look-alike letter, a full-width form, a ligature) are not a hidden channel and are out of scope; the look-alike check of the redactor handles copies of KNOWN values.
