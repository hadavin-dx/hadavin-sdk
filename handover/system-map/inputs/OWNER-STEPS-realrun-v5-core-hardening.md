## Order of the day

0. At least 3 hours before the start time: the studio lead messages you the start time (see "Present" in the Words box).
1. Before A: once the studio lead has told you the run name pattern, you pick your run name and send it (see the Words box). A: the studio lead sends the password-risk question and the numbers; you give two written answers: the risk answer and the money yes.
2. B: laptop settings. B1: the setup lines (only if the studio lead's session cannot reach your laptop). B2: the prep call (only if the studio lead says it happens), before C.
3. C: check the receiver mailbox (it is empty after B2), make the browser profile, n8n sign-in only if asked.
4. D: check what the film may show, and choose the proof screen. Before E you also receive the STOP LINE (step E).
5. E: launch the program; you type `y` and the confirmation line.
6. F: the film pause; the contact list is filmed first, then you type `end` (only when filming of it is done), then the program prints its list.
7. F2 (the program printed its list and you typed `end`): count this run's contacts with three searches (a, b, c), compare with the program's printed list, and only then delete them and the demo mails (after the studio lead says filming is finished and the copy-out is done; the **copy-out** is the studio lead saving a copy of what it needs from this run, for example the printed list and the counts, before you delete anything, because deletes cannot be undone; you do nothing for it, and the studio lead tells you by message when it is done).
8. After the run: G (only when the rule at the top of F2 sends you there: you did not type `end`, or the program printed no list; it uses the same three searches and the same count-first-then-delete order as F2), H (watch the edited video), I (clean-up), J (post).
