# Test standard (dry run only)
1. Plain words a non-technical reader understands.
2. Each sentence says who does what to whom (no vague passive claims like "is handled" or "is secured").
3. No unexplained labels: a word that could be read two ways (gate, queue, window, receipt, bank, approval) gets the missing noun or an explanation.
Severity: Blocker, Major, Nit. Report line numbers, the problem, the reason.
