## What this page is
The demo (lead-intake): a made-up sales lead goes into n8n (a tool that runs steps), is stored once, creates one contact in HubSpot (a sales-contact tool) and sends one mail; the take (one filmed run) shows that the same lead sent twice gives one mail and one contact write. The flow files carry placeholders for settings; a script (fill_flows.py, not built) fills them in. Below, the questions that must be answered first.

A row is open until its answer is written in this table's "Answer" column (the studio lead or the system-map session writes it, quoting who answered and when) (until a reply file is named, answers go directly in this column). Former items 3-19 and 22-25 (old numbering) are post-take or settled by the decisions of 16 Nov (which decision settles which row is in work/open-items-table-history-2.md); they are not tracked here. The "old #" column keeps the numbers other pages still cite. Every date below is a proposal by the system-map session (the Claude session that wrote this page); no one has agreed to it, and the studio lead accepts or changes each one.
