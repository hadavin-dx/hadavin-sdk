How to read the tags
- EST = my estimate of screen time, not measured.
- MEASURED = a number read by the lead from raw files, status MEASURED-ON-LAPTOP lead-read, quoted from V7-NUMBERS-lead-read with the row and test name. Raw files are laptop-local and I have not opened them.
- "None" in the number column means no measured row backs a number, so the scene carries no number.
- UNBACKED = the scene as first written has no evidence behind a caption; the row now gives the fallback.
- Wherever a wait is sped up, the screen must show the words "sped up" and the real wait next to them.
- Lease: film at lease 20 s and show "test setting: lease 20 s" on screen in every timed scene. The shipped lease is 120 s. The 45 s lease is not measured, so do not film at 45 s; if it is filmed anyway (or at 120 s), the captions must carry no measured number, or must say "at the 20 s test lease".
- Two different tests are both called K3. Demo K3 = n8n killed after the mail was delivered. Lane3 K3 = killed after the CRM write. Never mix them; this sheet uses demo K3 only, and says "lane3 K3" when it means the other.
