- M2 (section 3): overclaims about the demo's mutant tests: "the other 39 swap whole functions and would have to be rewritten as text pairs" and "its in-process guards already cover the 2 named faults and 66 more". The 29 text-replacement count is right (12 in test_mutants.py, 17 in test_mutants_more.py: weaken/schema_with/schema_without_unique_key call sites), but the other 39 are not mostly function-swap mutants: test_mutation_guards.py (20 tests) has one monkeypatch.setattr (line 86), the rest are plain boundary/state tests (test_source_ref_128_through_desk, test_requeue_age_boundary_direct, test_touch_only_for_the_owner...); test_mutants_more.py:31-165 has about 11 plain behaviour tests (test_email_key_strips_blanks, test_is_busy_takes_both_lock_codes, test_schema_version_refused...). So "68 mutant-style tests" is wrong: about 29 text-mutant tests plus a handful of setattr mutants. "66 more faults" unsupported. Verdict "NO as replacement, YES as addition" holds (in-process vs pytest subprocess on a temp copy), but "cost to rewrite 39" and "66 more" wording must be corrected.
