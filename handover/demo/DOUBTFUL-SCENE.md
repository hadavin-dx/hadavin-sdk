# The doubtful-case scene (a lead whose mail may or may not have gone out), 5 steps
Source: the v3 flows (n8n-flows-v3 / film-v3) and N8N-START.md. Marks: MEASURED = a raw file I was given shows it (none yet for v3: V7 raw files not named to me); READ-ONLY = I read it in the flow files, it was not run; NOT BUILT = does not exist yet. Made-up data, one machine, nothing independent.

1. A person sends one made-up lead through the form entry (the lead form webhook, loopback address, path mf-form). They see: the request goes in and n8n answers. Mark: READ-ONLY (v3 flows built; V7 runs the same entry on the stand-ins, raw files pending).
2. The mail step is made to fail AFTER the mail left (the SMTP server accepts the mail, then the connection drops before it says "queued"). Mark: NOT BUILT for the real take. The fault exists only in my SMTP stand-in (catcher drop mode, used by T12 and AF1); a real mailbox has no switch for it. The take needs either the stand-in on screen or a different way to cut the connection (not decided, not built).
3. The person sees the answer to the caller say outcome "unknown", not "done". Mark: READ-ONLY (OutUnknown node; exact reply text not re-checked on v3).
4. Within about 30 s (one recovery tick) the owner gets ONE alert e-mail, subject "[demo] Lead desk: mail outcome unknown for <lead id>", text "the mail may or may not have been sent. A person decides." The intake mailbox shows 0 or 1 mail with subject "[demo] lead <lead id>", and no second one appears after waiting longer than the lease. Mark: READ-ONLY for the alert and for "no resend" (design: unknown is excluded from recovery; the mail step refuses a second send). If the SMTP were down at that moment the alert would wait until it is back (retry with backoff, no cap): READ-ONLY, tested only by AF1, not run. A Slack alert instead of mail: NOT BUILT.
5. The person shows the table row for that lead: state unknown, mail_sent true, alert_sent true (false while an alert is still waiting). Mark: the row and its values are READ-ONLY (written by MarkUnknownA/B and AlertMark); a ready screen or saved query for the viewer is NOT BUILT (today it is a database query by hand).
