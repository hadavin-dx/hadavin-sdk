## PROBLEMS FIRST
M1 (Major; desk.py:786-787 after the patch, the hunk adds `exclusive=True` at about line 787): the one new flag turns a refused mirror call into a STOP for the whole copy pass; undocumented and untested. Verified in `_bounded` (desk.py:1445-1452): `exclusive=True` makes a second call with the same tag raise AdapterBusy immediately under `_worker_lock`; it never waits and never holds a slot while waiting: no deadlock; the cap (crm 8) and other kinds' throughput are unchanged. The mirror loop handles that refusal with `except AdapterBusy: return` (desk.py:786-787); the same `return` handles 'crm cap reached', so one lead's still-running abandoned call now ends the entire pass, not just that lead. Sequence: lead A is oldest by `changed_at` and its abandoned `set_fields` call is still hung (the adapter never returns); A's stage moves so the backoff entry no longer matches (`_MirrorWait` keyed by stage rank); every pass selects A first (`ORDER BY s.changed_at, s.lead_id LIMIT 5`), is refused, returns; leads B to E are never copied while A's call lives. Before the patch A got a second call, used its tries and left the selection after MIRROR_MAX_TRIES (5) failures; after the patch A's tries are never charged, so it stays at the head of the queue as long as the hang lasts. docs/limits.md:36-43 and :41 promise only 'a blind call for the same lead is not started again' and a row that is 'not claimed'; they do not say the mirror pass stops. The builder's section 4 admits 'the whole copy pass stops (same as at the worker cap today)': not the same: the cap is global, this is per lead. tests/test_codeant_desk.py only asserts `len(crm.started) == 1`, never that another lead still gets copied. Suggested fix: when the refusal is the tag rule, `continue` to the next row (pre-check `self._tagged.get(("crm", f"mirror:{lead_id}"))` under `_worker_lock`); keep `return` for the cap; add a test with two leads, A blocked and B copied. Severity conditional: needs a hung adapter, and the adapter has its own per-operation timeout that normally ends the call.
M2 (Major under rule 7, low real impact: stand-in server; demo_only/fake_hubspot.py): siblings of the N3-03 defect (dict read without the lock) are not fixed (reviewer read the full file at 558d37f): `do_GET` line 174 `st.by_email(ident)` iterates `self.contacts.items()` in a generator with no lock (a concurrent create or delete can raise 'dictionary changed size during iteration'); `_public()` (line 140), `cid in st.contacts` (175) and `_public(cid)` (176) are unlocked; the new `HubspotState.search` only protects the iteration: `_search` then calls `self._public(i)` after the lock is released, so a concurrent DELETE between the two raises KeyError and the handler dies: the patch moved the race a few lines down. The builder checked `sorted(st.contacts)` (164), safe; the `by_email` and `_public` sites were missed. Suggested fix: one locked 'public view' helper used by GET, search and PATCH.
