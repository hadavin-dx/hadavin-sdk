### BLOCKER-for-Windows (Major for merge): test_every_cure_in_the_message_works[crlf] FAILS when git has core.autocrlf=true
- Measured: GIT_CONFIG_GLOBAL=<file with core.autocrlf=true> pytest tests/test_hygiene.py on db54e13+v4: 1 failed, 30 passed; also on 2db3926+v4 (-k cure: 1 failed). Failure: `assert ['sonar-project.properties'] == []` after `git checkout -- <file>`.

- The cure itself is fine in the real repo under autocrlf=true: `.gitattributes` has `* text=auto eol=lf`; I clone with autocrlf=true: only scripts/*.ps1 and src/lead_desk/portal/icon.png hold CR; CRLF and lone-CR planted into sonar-project.properties, `git checkout -- sonar-project.properties` -> 0 CR bytes each.
- Fix (measured): write `.gitattributes` = `* text eol=lf` into the planted tree before `git add`. With it: 24 passed on Linux, 24 passed with autocrlf=true. Patch: /tmp/work/rv12-out/crlf-guard-v4-autocrlf-fix.patch (against db54e13, full replacement of v4, also folds the small trims below).
